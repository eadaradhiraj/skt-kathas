### Verses 1–2

* **Sanskrit:**
[शान्त-अग्नि-तुल्यं] [जल-हीन-समुद्र-तुल्यं] [प्रभा-हीन-सूर्य-तुल्यं] [शोभा-हीनं] [दिवङ्गतं] राज-शवं दृष्ट्वा कौसल्यायाः नेत्रेषु अश्रूणि [जातानि]।
[शोक-आकुलया] तया राज्ञः मस्तकम् अङ्के धृत्वा कैकेयी उक्ता।
* **English:** When Kausalya saw the lifeless body of the departed king, which was bereft of splendor like an extinguished fire, a waterless ocean, and a lusterless sun, her eyes filled with tears. Overcome with grief, she placed the king's head in her lap and spoke to Kaikeyi as follows:

---

### Verse 3

* **Sanskrit:**
कौसल्या - हे [दुराचारिणि] [क्रूरे] कैकेयि तव कामना [सफला] [जाता]।
इदानीं राजानम् अपि त्यक्त्वा [एकाग्र-चित्ता] त्वम् [अकण्टकं] राज्यं भुङ्क्ष्व।
* **English:** Kausalya - 'O wicked and cruel Kaikeyi! Look, your desire has been fulfilled. Now, having abandoned even the king, enjoy your undisputed kingdom with a single-minded focus.'

---

### Verse 4

* **Sanskrit:**
कौसल्या - रामः मां त्यक्त्वा वनं [गतः] मम स्वामी च स्वर्गं [गतः]।
इदानीं [दुर्गमे] मार्गे [सार्थ-हीना] [असहाया] [अबला-तुल्या] अहं जीवितुं न शक्नोमि।
* **English:** Kausalya - 'Rama has left me and gone to the forest, and my lord has departed to heaven. Now, like a helpless weak woman separated from her companions on a difficult path, I cannot survive.'

---

### Verse 5

* **Sanskrit:**
कौसल्या - [नारी-धर्म-त्यागिनीं] कैकेयीं विना संसारे अन्या का स्त्री [आराध्य-देव-स्वरूपं] पतिं त्यक्त्वा जीवितुम् इच्छेत्।
* **English:** Kausalya - 'Apart from Kaikeyi, who has abandoned the duty of a woman, which other woman in the world would wish to live after abandoning her husband, who is like a worshipable deity to her?'

---

### Verse 6

* **Sanskrit:**
कौसल्या - यथा कश्चित् [धन-लोभी] अन्येभ्यः विषं दत्त्वा [हत्या-जन्य-दोषेषु] ध्यानं न ददाति तथा एव अनया कैकेय्या मन्थरा-कारणात् इदं रघु-कुलं [नाशितम्]।
* **English:** Kausalya - 'Just as a greedy person poisons others and pays no attention to the sin of murder, similarly, this Kaikeyi has destroyed this lineage of the Raghus because of the hunchback Manthara.'

---

### Verse 7

* **Sanskrit:**
कौसल्या - कैकेय्या महाराजः [अयोग्य-कार्ये] [नियोजितः]।
तेन [पत्नी-सहितः] रामः वनं [प्रेषितः]।
यदा जनक-राजः इमं समाचारं श्रोष्यति तदा सः अपि [मत्-तुल्यः] [कष्ट-पीडितः] भविष्यति।
* **English:** Kausalya - 'Kaikeyi engaged the king in an improper act and caused him to send Rama to the forest along with his wife. When King Janaka hears this news, he too will suffer greatly, just like me.'

---

### Verse 8

* **Sanskrit:**
कौसल्या - अहम् [अनाथा] [विधवा] च [जाता]।
इयं वार्ता मम [धर्मात्मना] [कमल-नयनेन] पुत्रेण रामेण [अज्ञाता]।
सः तु [जीवन्] एव इतः [अदृश्यः] [जातः]।
* **English:** Kausalya - 'I have become an orphan and a widow—this fact is unknown to my righteous, lotus-eyed son Rama. He has disappeared from here while still alive.'

---

### Verse 9

* **Sanskrit:**
कौसल्या - [पति-सेवा-रूप-मनोहर-तपस्विनी] [विदेह-राज-कुमारी] सीता [दुःख-योग्या] न अस्ति।
सा वने दुःखम् अनुभूय [उद्विग्ना] भविष्यति।
* **English:** Kausalya - 'Sita, the princess of Videha, who performs the beautiful penance of serving her husband, does not deserve to suffer. Experiencing hardships in the forest, she will become highly distressed.'

---

### Verse 10

* **Sanskrit:**
कौसल्या - रात्रौ [भयानक-शब्द-कारिणां] पशु-पक्षिणां ध्वनिं श्रुत्वा [भय-भीता] सीता रामस्य एव शरणं गमिष्यति।
तस्य अङ्के एव गत्वा सा [निलीना] भविष्यति।
* **English:** Kausalya - 'Hearing the sounds of terrifying animals and birds at night, a terrified Sita will seek refuge only with Rama. She will hide in his lap.'

---

### Verse 11

* **Sanskrit:**
कौसल्या - [वृद्धः] [कन्या-मात्र-सन्ततिः] सः राजा जनकः अपि सीतायाः एव वारं-वारं चिन्तां [कुर्वन्] [शोक-मग्नः] [सन्] अवश्यम् एव स्व-प्राणान् त्यक्ष्यति।
* **English:** Kausalya - 'King Janaka, who has grown old and has only daughters as his offspring, will surely give up his life, overwhelmed with grief while repeatedly worrying about Sita.'

---

### Verse 12

* **Sanskrit:**
कौसल्या - अहम् अपि अद्य एव मृत्युं वृणोमि।
[पतिव्रता-तुल्या] अहं पत्युः शरीरम् आलिङ्ग्य चिता-अग्नौ प्रवेशं करिष्यामि।
* **English:** Kausalya - 'I too shall embrace death this very day. Like a devoted wife, I will embrace my husband's body and enter the fire of the funeral pyre.'

---

### Verse 13

* **Sanskrit:**
पत्युः शरीरं हृदयेन संलग्नीकृत्य करुण-विलापं [कुर्वती] [अत्यन्त-दुःख-आर्ता] [तपस्विनी] कौसल्या [राज-कार्य-दर्शिभिः] मन्त्रिभिः अन्यासां स्त्रीणां साहाय्येन ततः [दूरी-कृता]।
* **English:** The ministers overseeing state affairs had the ascetic Kausalya, who was deeply afflicted by extreme grief and wailing piteously while clutching her husband's body to her heart, removed from there by other women.

---

### Verse 14

* **Sanskrit:**
ततः तैः महाराजस्य शरीरं [तैल-पूर्णायां] नौकायां संस्थाप्य वसिष्ठ-आदीनाम् आज्ञा-अनुसारं [शव-रक्षा-आदीनाम्] [अन्येषां] राजकीय-कार्याणां प्रबन्धनम् [आरब्धम्]।
* **English:** Then, placing the king's body in a boat filled with oil, they commenced the management of the body's preservation and all other royal duties in accordance with the commands of Vashistha and others.

---

### Verse 15

* **Sanskrit:**
ते [सर्वज्ञाः] मन्त्रिणः पुत्रं विना राज्ञः दाह-संस्कारं कर्तुं न [समर्थाः] [जाताः]।
अतः तैः तस्य शवस्य रक्षा [आरब्धा]।
* **English:** Those omniscient ministers could not perform the cremation of the king without his son, and therefore, they began to preserve his corpse.

---

### Verse 16

* **Sanskrit:**
यदा मन्त्रिभिः राज्ञः शवः तैल-नौकायां [शायितः] तदा इदं ज्ञात्वा हा एते महाराजाः [परलोक-वासिनः] [जाताः] इति [कथयन्तीभिः] [सर्वाभिः] राज्ञीभिः पुनः विलापः [आरब्धः]।
* **English:** When the ministers laid the king's body in the oil boat, realizing this, all the queens began to lament again, saying, 'Alas! The great king has departed to the other world.'

---

### Verse 17

* **Sanskrit:**
तासां मुखेषु नेत्रेभ्यः अश्रूणां निर्झराः [पतिताः]।
ताभिः स्व-भुजौ उपरि उत्थाप्य [दीनाभिः] [शोक-सन्तप्ताभिः] च भूत्वा [दयनीयः] विलापः [कृतः]।
* **English:** Streams of tears were flowing from their eyes over their faces. Raising their arms upwards, they, tormented by grief, wept piteously and engaged in a miserable lamentation.

---

### Verse 18

* **Sanskrit:**
राज्ञ्यः - हा महाराज वयं [सत्य-प्रतिज्ञेन] [सदा-प्रिय-वादिना] स्व-पुत्रेण रामेण [विहीनाः] [स्थिताः] एव।
इदानीं भवता अपि किमर्थं वयं [त्यज्यमानाः]।
* **English:** The Queens - 'Alas, Great King! We were already separated from our son Rama, who is true to his vows and always speaks sweetly. Why are you too abandoning us now?'

---

### Verse 19

* **Sanskrit:**
राज्ञ्यः - रामेण [वियुक्ताः] [विधवाः] वयं [सर्वाः] अस्याः [दुष्ट-विचार-वत्याः] सपत्न्याः कैकेय्याः समीपे कथं निवत्स्यामः।
* **English:** The Queens - 'Separated from Rama, how will all of us widows live near this evil-minded co-wife Kaikeyi?'

---

### Verse 20

* **Sanskrit:**
राज्ञ्यः - यः अस्माकं भवतः च रक्षकः प्रभुः [जातः] सः [मनस्वी] रामचन्द्रः राज-लक्ष्मीं त्यक्त्वा वनं [गतः]।
* **English:** The Queens - 'The noble-minded Ramachandra, who was the protector and lord of both us and you, has left for the forest, abandoning royal splendor.'

---

### Verse 21

* **Sanskrit:**
राज्ञ्यः - वीर-वरेण रामेण भवता च विना अस्माकम् उपरि [महत्] [सङ्कटम्] [आगतम्]।
तेन वयं [मोहिताः] भवामः।
इदानीं सपत्न्या कैकेय्या [तिरस्कृताः] वयम् अत्र कथं निवत्स्यामः।
* **English:** The Queens - 'In the absence of the great hero Rama and yourself, a massive crisis has fallen upon us, by which we are bewildered. How will we be able to live here now, being humiliated by our co-wife Kaikeyi?'

---

### Verse 22

* **Sanskrit:**
राज्ञ्यः - यया राज्ञः [सीता-सहितस्य] रामस्य [महा-बलस्य] लक्ष्मणस्य अपि परित्यागः [कृतः] सा अन्यस्य कस्य त्यागं न करिष्यति।
* **English:** The Queens - 'She who has abandoned the king, Rama along with Sita, and the immensely strong Lakshmana, who else will she not abandon?'

---

### Verse 23

* **Sanskrit:**
रघु-कुल-नरेशस्य दशरथस्य ताभिः [सुन्दरीभिः] राज्ञीभिः [महा-शोक-ग्रस्ताभिः] अश्रूणि [स्रावयन्तीभिः] [नाना-प्रकाराः] चेष्टाः [कृताः] विलापः च [कृतः]।
तासाम् आनन्दः [नष्टः]।
* **English:** Those beautiful queens of Dasharatha, the king of the Raghu dynasty, gripped by immense grief and shedding tears, performed various actions and lamented. Their joy was lost.

---

### Verse 24

* **Sanskrit:**
[महा-मनसा] राज्ञा दशरथेन [हीना] सा अयोध्या-पुरी [नक्षत्र-हीन-रात्रि-तुल्या] [पति-विहीन-नारी-तुल्या] [श्री-हीना] [जाता]।
* **English:** Bereft of the noble-minded King Dasharatha, that city of Ayodhya became devoid of splendor, like a starless night and a husbandless woman.

---

### Verse 25

* **Sanskrit:**
नगरस्य [सर्वैः] मनुष्यैः अश्रूणि [स्रावितानि]।
[कुल-वतीभिः] स्त्रीभिः हाहा-कारः [कृतः]।
चतुष्पथाः गृहाणां द्वाराणि च [शून्यानि] [दृष्टानि]।
एवं सा पुरी [पूर्व-तुल्यां] शोभां न [प्राप्ता]।
* **English:** All the men of the city were shedding tears. The noble women were making loud wails. The crossroads and the doors of the houses appeared desolate. Thus, that city did not possess its former beauty.

---

### Verse 26

* **Sanskrit:**
[शोक-वशी-भूतः] राजा दशरथः स्वर्गं [गतः]।
तस्य राज्ञ्यः शोकात् एव भू-तले [पतिताः]।
अस्मिन् शोके एव सहसा सूर्यस्य किरणानां प्रचारः [अवरुद्धः]।
सूर्य-देवः [अस्तङ्गतः]।
तत्-पश्चात् अन्धकारस्य प्रचारं [कुर्वती] रात्रिः [उपस्थिता]।
* **English:** Overcome by grief, King Dasharatha departed to heaven, and his queens, stricken by sorrow, fell to the ground. Amidst this grief, the spreading of the sun's rays suddenly stopped, and the sun god set. Thereafter, the night, spreading darkness, arrived.

---

### Verse 27

* **Sanskrit:**
तत्र [आगतैः] सुहृद्भिः कञ्चित् अपि पुत्रं विना राज्ञः दाह-संस्कारः न [रोचितः]।
इदानीं राज्ञः दर्शनम् [अचिन्त्यं] [जातम्] इति [चिन्तयद्भिः] तैः [सर्वैः] तस्मिन् [तैल-पूर्णे] कटाहे तस्य शवः [सुरक्षितः]।
* **English:** The well-wishers present there did not approve of the king's cremation taking place without any of his sons. Thinking that the king's sight had now become unimaginable, they all kept his corpse safe in that oil-filled cauldron.

---

### Verse 28

* **Sanskrit:**
सूर्यं विना [प्रभा-हीन-आकाश-तुल्या] नक्षत्राणि विना [शोभा-हीन-रात्रि-तुल्या] च अयोध्या-पुरी [महात्मना] राज्ञा दशरथेन [रहिता] [श्री-हीना] [प्रतीता]।
तस्याः मार्गेषु चतुष्पथेषु च [अश्रु-अवरुद्ध-कण्ठानां] मनुष्याणां सम्मर्दः [समवेतः]।
* **English:** Like a lusterless sky without the sun and a beauty-less night without the stars, the city of Ayodhya, bereft of the great-souled King Dasharatha, appeared devoid of splendor. On its streets and crossroads, crowds of people with throats choked with tears had assembled.

---

### Verse 29

* **Sanskrit:**
स्त्रीणां पुरुषाणां च समूहैः एकत्र [समवेतैः] भूत्वा भरत-मातुः कैकेय्याः निन्दा [कृता]।
तस्मिन् समये महाराजस्य मृत्युना अयोध्या-पुर्यां [निवसद्भिः] [सर्वैः] जनैः [शोक-आकुलैः] [भूतम्]।
केन अपि शान्तिः न [प्राप्ता]।
* **English:** Flocks of men and women, standing together, condemned Bharata's mother, Kaikeyi. At that time, due to the death of the great king, all the people living in the city of Ayodhya were overwhelmed with grief. No one found peace.
