### Verse 1

**Sanskrit:**
अयोध्यायां [सर्वेषां] जनानां सा रात्री रोदनेन [व्यतीता]। तस्यां [हर्ष-हीनायां] रात्र्याम् आनन्दस्य लेशः अपि न [दृष्टः]। जनानां कण्ठाः [अश्रु-पूर्णाः] [जाताः]। [महतः] दुःखात् सा रात्री [सर्वेभ्यः] [अतीव-दीर्घा] [अनुभूता]॥१॥

**English:**
In Ayodhya, that night of the people passed only in weeping and wailing. In it, there was not even a trace of joy. The throats of all the people were choked with tears. Due to great sorrow, that night seemed exceedingly long to everyone.

---

### Verse 2

**Sanskrit:**
रात्र्यां [व्यतीतायां] सूर्ये च [उदिते] सति [राज्य-प्रबन्ध-कर्तारो] ब्राह्मणाः [एकत्र-भूताः] राज-सभां [गताः]॥२॥

**English:**
When the night had passed and the sun had risen, the Brahmins managing the affairs of the state gathered and went to the royal assembly.

---

### Verses 3–4

**Sanskrit:**
मार्कण्डेय-मौद्गल्य-वामदेव-कश्यप-कात्यायन-गौतम-जाबालि-मुनयो मन्त्रिभिः सह [राज-पुरोहितस्य] वसिष्ठस्य पुरतः [उपविष्टाः]। तैः [ब्राह्मण-श्रेष्ठैः] [स्वकीया] [पृथक्-पृथक्] मतिः [प्रकटी-कृता]॥३-४॥

**English:**
The sages Markandeya, Maudgalya, Vamadeva, Kashyapa, Katyayana, Gautama, and the highly illustrious Jabali—all these excellent Brahmins sat before the royal priest Vashistha and, together with the ministers, expressed their individual opinions.

---

### Verse 5

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - पुत्र-शोकेन महाराजस्य स्वर्ग-वासि-कारणाद् इयं रात्री [महता] दुःखेन [व्यतीता]। अस्माभिः सा रात्री [शत-वर्ष-समाना] [अनुभूता]॥५॥

**English:**
The Brahmins spoke: "Due to the Emperor departing to heaven out of grief for his son, this night passed with great sorrow, which felt to us like a hundred years."

---

### Verse 6

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - महाराज-दशरथः स्वर्गं [गतः]। श्री-राम-चन्द्रो वन-वासी [जातः]। [तेजस्वी] लक्ष्मणः अपि श्री-रामेण सह [निर्गतः]॥६॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "Emperor Dasharatha has gone to heaven, Shri Ramachandra has become a forest-dweller, and the radiant Lakshmana has also departed along with Shri Rama."

---

### Verse 7

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - [शत्रु-सन्ताप-करौ] भ्रातरौ भरत-शत्रुघ्नौ केकय-देशस्य [रमणीये] राज-गृहे माता-महस्य भवने निवसतः॥७॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "Both brothers, Bharata and Shatrughna, tormentors of enemies, reside in their maternal grandfather's house in the delightful palace of the Kekeya country."

---

### Verse 8

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - [इक्ष्वाकु-वंशीयेषु] राज-कुमारेषु कश्चिद् अद्य एव अत्रत्यो राजा [कर्तव्यः]। राजानं विना अस्माकम् एतस्य राज्यस्य नाशो भविष्यति॥८॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "Someone among the princes of the Ikshvaku lineage must be made king here this very day, for without a king, this kingdom of ours will be ruined."

---

### Verse 9

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - यस्मिन् जन-पदे राजा न भवति तत्र [विद्युन्-मालालङ्कृतो] [महा-गर्जन-कारी] मेघः पृथिव्यां [दिव्यस्य] जलस्य वर्षां न करोति॥९॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "In a land where there is no king, the cloud adorned with garlands of lightning and roaring loudly does not shower divine water upon the earth."

---

### Verse 10

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - यस्मिन् जन-पदे राजा न वर्तते तत्र क्षेत्रेषु [मुष्टि-प्रमाणानि] बीजानि न वप्यन्ते। [राज-रहिते] देशे पुत्रः पितुः स्त्री च पत्युः वशे न तिष्ठति॥१०॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "In a territory where there is no king, handfuls of seeds are not sown in the fields. In a kingless land, a son does not remain obedient to his father, nor a wife to her husband."

---

### Verse 11

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - [राज-हीने] देशे धनं [स्वकीयं] न भवति। [राज-रहिते] राज्ये पत्नी अपि [स्वकीया] न तिष्ठति। [राज-शून्ये] देशे इदं [महद्] भयं सदा वर्तते। तत्र [अन्यत्] सत्यं कथं तिष्ठेत्॥११॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "In a kingless country, wealth does not remain one's own. In a land without a king, even a wife does not remain one's own. In a king-devoid land, this great fear constantly persists; how then could any other truth endure there?"

---

### Verse 12

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - [राज-रहिते] राज्ये मानवाः [किमपि] सभा-भवनं न कारयन्ति। ते [रमणीयम्] उद्यानम् अपि न कारयन्ति। [हर्षोत्साह-सम्पन्नाः] जनाः पुण्य-गृहाणि अपि न रचयन्ति॥१२॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "In a kingdom without a king, people do not have assembly halls built. They do not cause delightful gardens to be laid out, nor do people filled with joy and enthusiasm erect sacred buildings."

---

### Verse 13

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - यत्र राजा न वर्तते तस्मिन् जन-पदे [यज्ञ-शीलाः] द्विजाः [कठोर-व्रत-पालकाः] [जितेन्द्रियाः] ब्राह्मणाः [महतां] [सर्व-यजमान-सर्वर्त्विक्-युक्तानाम्] यज्ञानाम् अनुष्ठानं न कुर्वन्ति॥१३॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "Where there is no king, in that territory, the naturally sacrifice-performing twice-born and the self-controlled Brahmins observing severe vows do not perform those grand sacrifices wherein all are both patrons and priests."

---

### Verse 14

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - [राज-रहिते] जन-पदे यदि कदाचित् [महतां] यज्ञानाम् आरम्भः भवति तदा [धन-सम्पन्नाः] ब्राह्मणाः अपि ऋत्विग्भ्यः [पर्याप्तां] दक्षिणां न प्रयच्छन्ति। जनाः अस्मान् [धनिकान्] मत्वा लुण्ठेयुः इति तेषां भयं वर्तते॥१४॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "In a kingless land, even if grand sacrifices happen to be commenced, wealthy Brahmins do not grant adequate sacrificial gifts to the priests, fearing that people might deem them rich and plunder them."

---

### Verse 15

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - [अराजके] देशे [राष्ट्रोन्नति-कराः] [प्रहृष्ट-नट-नर्तक-कला-प्रदर्शन-युक्ताः] उत्सवाः न वर्धन्ते। तत्र [अन्ये] [राष्ट्र-हित-कारिणः] सङ्घाः अपि वृद्धिं न गच्छन्ति॥१५॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "In an anarchic land, nation-uplifting festivals featuring the art performances of overjoyed actors and dancers do not flourish, nor do other nation-benefiting associations thrive."

---

### Verse 16

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - [राज-रहिते] राज्ये वादि-प्रतिवादिनोः विवादस्य [सन्तोष-प्रदः] निर्णयः न भवति। वणिजः लाभं न प्राप्नुवन्ति। [कथा-श्रवणेच्छुकाः] जनाः [कथा-वाचकानां] पौराणिकानां कथाभिः [प्रसन्नाः] न भवन्ति॥१६॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "In a kingless kingdom, no satisfactory settlement is reached in disputes between plaintiffs and defendants, merchants earn no profit, and people eager to listen to sacred legends are not pleased by the narrations of the storytellers."

---

### Verse 17

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - [राज-रहिते] जन-पदे [स्वर्णाभूषण-विभूषिताः] [सङ्गताः] कुमार्यः सन्ध्या-काले उद्यानेषु क्रीडितुं न गच्छन्ति॥१७॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "In a kingless territory, maidens adorned with golden ornaments do not gather in the evening to go and play in the gardens."

---

### Verse 18

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - [राज-रहिते] राज्ये [धनवन्तः] जनाः [सुरक्षिताः] न तिष्ठन्ति। [कृषि-गोरक्षा-जीविनः] वैश्याः अपि द्वारम् उद्घाट्य शयितुं न शक्नुवन्ति॥१८॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "In a kingdom without a king, wealthy people do not remain secure, nor can the Vaishyas living by agriculture and cattle-rearing sleep with their doors open."

---

### Verse 19

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - [राज-रहिते] जन-पदे [कामिनः] मानवाः नारीभिः सह [शीघ्र-गामिभिः] वाहनैः वन-विहाराय न निर्गच्छन्ति॥१९॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "In a territory devoid of a king, pleasure-seeking men do not venture out into the forests with women in swift vehicles for pleasure-trips."

---

### Verse 20

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - यत्र राजा न भवति तस्मिन् जन-पदे [षष्टि-वर्ष-वयस्काः] [दन्त-युक्ताः] [बद्ध-घण्टाः] गजाः राज-मार्गेषु न भ्रमन्ति॥२०॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "Where there is no king, in that land sixty-year-old tusked elephants with bells tied to them do not roam along the royal highways."

---

### Verse 21

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - [राज-रहिते] राज्ये धनुर्-विद्याभ्यास-काले [निरन्तर-बाण-क्षेपकाणां] वीराणां प्रत्यञ्चा-करतलयोः शब्दः न श्रूयते॥२१॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "In a kingdom without a king, during the practice of archery, the sound of the bowstrings and clapping palms of warriors continuously shooting arrows at targets is not heard."

---

### Verse 22

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - [राज-रहिते] जन-पदे [दूर-गामिनः] वणिजः [बहूनि] विक्रय-वस्तूनि गृहीत्वा [कुशल-पूर्वकं] मार्गं तरितुं न शक्नुवन्ति॥२२॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "In a land devoid of a king, merchants traveling far with abundant goods for sale cannot traverse their routes in safety."

---

### Verse 23

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - यत्र राजा न भवति तस्मिन् जन-पदे यत्र सन्ध्या भवति तत्र एव [कृत-निवासः] अन्तः-करणेन परमात्मानं [ध्यायन्] [एकाकी] [विचरन्] [जितेन्द्रियः] मुनिः भोजन-प्रदात्रभावे न भ्रमति॥२३॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "Where there is no king, in that land a self-controlled sage who camps wherever evening falls, meditates upon the Supreme Soul in his inner heart, and wanders alone, does not roam, for there is no one to offer him alms."

---

### Verse 24

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - [अराजके] देशे जनानाम् [अप्राप्तानां] वस्तूनां प्राप्तिः [प्राप्तानां] च वस्तूनां रक्षा न भवति। राज्ञः अभावे सेना अपि युद्धे शत्रूणां सम्मुखे न तिष्ठति॥२४॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "In an anarchic land, people achieve neither the acquisition of unobtained possessions nor the protection of what is already acquired. In the absence of a king, even the army does not confront enemies in battle."

---

### Verse 25

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - [राज-रहिते] राज्ये [वस्त्राभूषण-भूषिताः] जनाः [हृष्ट-पुष्टैः] [उत्तमैः] अश्वैः रथैः च सहसा यात्रां न सदा-लुण्ठक-भयात् कुर्वन्ति॥२५॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "In a kingdom without a king, people adorned in fine garments and jewels do not travel openly with well-fed, excellent horses and chariots, because they constantly fear highwaymen."

---

### Verse 26

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - [राज-रहिते] राज्ये [शास्त्र-विशारदाः] मानवाः वनेषु उपवनेषु च शास्त्राणि [व्याकुर्वन्तः] स्थातुं न शक्नुवन्ति॥२६॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "In a kingdom devoid of a king, scholars eminent in the scriptures cannot dwell peacefully in forests and groves while expounding the sacred texts."

---

### Verse 27

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - यत्र अराजकता प्रसरति तस्मिन् जन-पदे [नियत-चित्ताः] जनाः देव-पूजायै पुष्पाणां मिष्टान्नानां दक्षिणानां च व्यवस्थां न कुर्वन्ति॥२७॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "Where lawlessness spreads, in that land even self-restrained people do not arrange flowers, sweets, and offerings for worshiping the deities."

---

### Verse 28

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - यस्मिन् जन-पदे राजा न भवति तत्र [चन्दनागुरु-लिप्ताः] राज-कुमाराः वसन्त-काले [विकसिताः] वृक्षाः इव न शोभन्ते॥२८॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "In a land where there is no king, princes anointed with sandalwood and aloe do not shine like blooming trees in the springtime."

---

### Verse 29

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - यथा [जल-हीनाः] नद्यः [तृण-हीनं] वनं [गोपाल-रहिताः] गावः च न शोभन्ते तथा [राज-रहितं] राज्यं न शोभते॥२९॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "Just as waterless rivers, grassless forests, and herds of cows without a cowherd do not look glorious, similarly a kingdom without a king has no splendor."

---

### Verse 30

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - यथा ध्वजः रथस्य ज्ञानं कारयति धूमः च अग्नेः [बोधकः] भवति तथा [राज-कार्य-द्रष्टॄणाम्] अस्माकम् अधिकारस्य [प्रकाशकः] महाराजः इतः देव-लोकं [गतः]॥३०॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "Just as a banner indicates a chariot and smoke indicates fire, so the Emperor who illuminated the authority of us who oversee state affairs has departed hence to the celestial realm."

---

### Verse 31

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - राज्ञः अभावे राज्ये कस्य अपि मानवस्य किम् अपि वस्तु [स्वकीयं] न तिष्ठति। यथा मत्स्याः परस्परं भक्षयन्ति तथा [अराजके] देशे जनाः सदा परस्परं लुण्ठन्ति॥३१॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "In the absence of a king, no object of any human remains their own in the kingdom. Just as fish devour one another, so in a kingless country people perpetually rob and prey upon one another."

---

### Verse 32

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - ये [वेद-शास्त्र-वर्णाश्रम-मर्यादा-भञ्जकाः] नास्तिकाः पूर्वं [राज-दण्ड-पीडिताः] [दमिताः] [स्थिताः] ते अपि अधुना राज्ञः अभावे [निःशङ्काः] भूत्वा [स्वकीयं] प्रभुत्वं प्रकटी-करिष्यन्ति॥३२॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "Those atheists who violate the bounds of the Vedas, scriptures, and social orders, who were formerly held suppressed through royal punishment, will now also grow fearless in the king's absence and manifest their dominance."

---

### Verse 33

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - यथा दृष्टिः सदा शरीरस्य हिते [प्रवृत्ता] भवति तथा राजा राज्यस्य मध्ये [सत्य-धर्म-प्रवर्तकः] भवति॥३३॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "Just as sight is ever engaged in the welfare of the body, so is the king the promoter of truth and righteousness within the kingdom."

---

### Verse 34

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - राजा एव सत्यं धर्मः च वर्तते। राजा एव [कुलवतां] कुलं भवति। राजा एव माता पिता तथा च मानवानां [हित-करः] अस्ति॥३४॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "The king alone is truth and righteousness. The king alone is the noble lineage of the highborn. The king alone is mother and father, and the king alone is the benefactor of mankind."

---

### Verse 35

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - राजा [स्वेन] [महता] चरित्रेण यम-कुबेरेन्द्र-महाबल-वरुणेभ्यः अपि [श्रेष्ठतरः] भवति। यमः केवलं दण्डस्य कुबेरः धनस्य इन्द्रः पालनस्य वरुणः च सदाचारस्य नियन्त्रणं करोति। परन्तु [उत्तमे] राज्ञि एते चत्वारः गुणाः विद्यन्ते अतः सः एतेभ्यः [विशिष्टः] भवति॥३५॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "Through his exalted character, a king surpasses even Yama, Kubera, Indra, and the mighty Varuna. Yama administers only punishment, Kubera only wealth, Indra only protection, and Varuna only moral restraint; but in an excellent king, all four of these attributes are present, and therefore he excels them."

### Verse 36

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - यदि संसारे [शुभाशुभ-विभाग-कर्ता] राजा न स्यात् तर्हि इदं [समग्रं] जगत् [अन्धकाराच्छन्नम्] इव भवेत्। तदा किमपि न दृश्येत॥३६॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "If there were no king in the world discerning between good and evil, then this entire world would become as if shrouded in darkness, and nothing at all would be discernible."

### Verse 37

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - हे वसिष्ठ यथा [उद्वेलितः] समुद्रः [स्वकीयां] तट-भूमिं प्राप्य ततः अग्रे न वर्धते तथा महाराजस्य जीवन-काले अपि अस्माभिः सर्वैः केवलं भवतः एव वचसः उल्लङ्घनं न [कृतम्]॥३७॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "O Vashistha! Just as the swelling sea reaches its shoreline and advances no further, even so during the lifetime of the Emperor, none of us ever transgressed your word alone."

### Verse 38

**Sanskrit:**
ब्राह्मणाः - अतः हे [विप्र-वर] अस्मिन् समये अस्माकं व्यवहारं दृष्ट्वा राज्ञः अभावे [अरण्य-भूतम्] इमं देशं विलोक्य भवान् एव [कञ्चित्] [इक्ष्वाकु-वंशीयं] राज-कुमारम् अथवा [अन्यं] [कञ्चित्] [योग्यं] नरं राज-पदे [अभिषिक्तं] करोतु॥३८॥

**English:**
The Brahmins said: "Therefore, O best of Brahmins! Beholding our demeanor at this time and looking upon this land which has turned into a wilderness in the absence of a king, you yourself anoint some prince of the Ikshvaku lineage, or some other worthy man, to the throne of kingship."
