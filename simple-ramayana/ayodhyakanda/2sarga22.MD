### **वाल्मीकिरामायणे अयोध्याकाण्डे द्वाविंशः सर्गः (Sarga 22)**

---

#### **श्लोकौ १–२ (Verses 1–2)**

ततः [आत्मवान्] रामः धैर्येण स्वमनः [स्थिरं] कृत्वा, [दुःखाकुलं], महासर्पम् इव [श्वसन्तं], क्रोधेन [विशालनयनं], स्वस्य [प्रियं] भ्रातरं सुहृदं च लक्ष्मणम् उपगम्य एवम् —
Then self-possessed Rāma, steadying his composure with fortitude, approached his dear brother and intimate friend Lakṣmaṇa—who was wretched with anguish, hissing like a lordly serpent, his eyes widened in wrath—and spoke to him as follows:

---

#### **श्लोकः ३ (Verse 3)**

श्रीरामः — "त्वं कोपं दुःखं च नियन्त्रय, केवलं धीरतां भज। एतम् अपमानं दूरे त्यक्त्वा [परमम्] आनन्दं स्वीकुरु।"
Shri Rama said — "Restrain both anger and grief; take refuge only in steadfastness. Casting aside this sense of humiliation, hold fast to a higher joy."

---

#### **श्लोकः ४ (Verse 4)**

श्रीरामः — "मम राज्याभिषेककार्यम् अद्य यत् सर्वं [सज्जीकृतम्] अस्ति, तत् सर्वम् अधुना एव [स्थगितं] कुरु। शीघ्रं कार्यं कुरु, यत् च [करणीयम्] अस्ति तत् सर्वं निर्विघ्नेन निष्पादय।"
Shri Rama said — "Whatever has been arranged today for my consecration—set it all aside at once. Act swiftly, and do what must be done without mishap."

---

#### **श्लोकः ५ (Verse 5)**

श्रीरामः — "हे सौमित्रे (सुमित्रापुत्र लक्ष्मण), मम राज्याभिषेकस्य कृते यः उत्साहः प्रयत्नः च [कृतः], अधुना तस्य अभिषेकस्य स्थगनाय अपि सः एव उत्साहः भवतु।"
Shri Rama said — "O Saumitra (Lakshmana), let the same eager effort that prepared my coronation now be applied to bringing those preparations to an end."

---

#### **श्लोकः ६ (Verse 6)**

श्रीरामः — "मम राज्याभिषेकस्य वार्तया [सन्तप्तमनस्का] मम माता (कैकेयी) यथा पुनः [संशयग्रस्ता] न भवेत्, तथा त्वं कुरु।"
Shri Rama said — "Act so that my mother—whose heart burned at the prospect of my consecration—does not remain troubled by doubt."

---

#### **श्लोकः ७ (Verse 7)**

श्रीरामः — "हे सौमित्रे (सुमित्रापुत्र लक्ष्मण), तस्याः हृदये संशयात् [जातं] तत् दुःखं क्षणमात्रम् अपि उपेक्षितुं मया न शक्यते।"
Shri Rama said — "O Saumitra (Lakshmana), I cannot bring myself to ignore—even for a moment—the sorrow born of suspicion that has arisen in her heart."

---

#### **श्लोकः ८ (Verse 8)**

श्रीरामः — "[जानता] [अजानता] वा मया मम मातॄणां पितुः वा [अप्रियकरम्] अणुकृत्यम् अपि कदापि [कृतम्] इति अहं न स्मरामि।"
Shri Rama said — "I do not recall ever—whether knowingly or through heedlessness—having done here even the slightest thing that displeased my mothers or my father."

---

#### **श्लोकः ९ (Verse 9)**

श्रीरामः — "[सत्यवादी], सदा [सत्यनिष्ठः], पराक्रमे [धीरः], परलोकात् अधर्मात् च [भीतः] मम पिता अधुना [निर्भयः] भवतु।"
Shri Rama said — "May my father—truthful, ever devoted to truth, steadfast in valor—who once feared the other world and its moral consequence, now be free from fear."

---

#### **श्लोकः १० (Verse 10)**

श्रीरामः — "यदि एतत् मम राज्याभिषेककार्यं न निवर्त्यते, तर्हि 'मम सत्यं न [पूरितम्]' इति चिन्तया मम पिता अपि [सन्तप्तः] भविष्यति। पितुः सः एव सन्तापः माम् अपि पीडयिष्यति।"
Shri Rama said — "For if this act—the preparations for my consecration—is not withdrawn, my father too will be scorched by the anguish, 'My truth has not been fulfilled,' and that very torment of his will afflict me as well."

---

#### **श्लोकः ११ (Verse 11)**

श्रीरामः — "अतः हे लक्ष्मण, राज्याभिषेकसज्जतां निवर्तय। ततः अनन्तरम् एव अहम् इतः वनं गन्तुम् इच्छामि।"
Shri Rama said — "Therefore, O Lakshmana, have the arrangements for the consecration withdrawn; only after that do I wish to depart from here to the forest."

---

#### **श्लोकः १२ (Verse 12)**

श्रीरामः — "अद्य मम वनवासाय प्रस्थानात् अनन्तरम्, [सिद्धमनोरथा] राजपुत्री (कैकेयी) निःशङ्कं स्वपुत्रस्य भरतस्य राज्याभिषेकं कारयिष्यति।"
Shri Rama said — "Once I depart into exile today, the king's daughter—her aim accomplished—will then, without hesitation, have her son Bharata consecrated."

---

#### **श्लोकः १३ (Verse 13)**

श्रीरामः — "[वल्कलमृगाजिनधारी] [जटामण्डलधारी] च अहं यदा वनं गमिष्यामि, तदा एव कैकेयी मनःशान्तिं प्राप्स्यति।"
Shri Rama said — "When I, clad in bark-cloth and antelope skin and bearing a circlet of matted hair, have gone to the forest, then Kaikeyi will at last have peace of mind."

---

#### **श्लोकः १४ (Verse 14)**

श्रीरामः — "एतं निश्चयं [कृतवतः] मम मनः [सुस्थिरम्] अस्ति, अतः अहम् तं (दशरथम्) न व्यथयेयम्। अहम् अविलम्बेन वनवासं गमिष्यामि।"
Shri Rama said — "Since this resolve has been formed and my mind is well-composed, I ought not to distress him; I shall depart into exile without delay."

---

#### **श्लोकः १५ (Verse 15)**

श्रीरामः — "हे सौमित्रे (सुमित्रापुत्र लक्ष्मण), मम वनवासे, [दत्तस्य] राज्यस्य निवृत्तौ च केवलम् दैवम् एव कारणम् इति ज्ञातव्यम्।"
Shri Rama said — "O Saumitri (Lakshmana), in my exile—and in the revocation of the kingdom that had been granted—destiny alone should be recognized as the cause."

---

#### **श्लोकः १६ (Verse 16)**

श्रीरामः — "यदि एषा मनोवृत्तिः एतत् व्यसनम् च [दैवनिर्दिष्टम्] न भवेत्, तर्हि कैकेयी माम् पीडयितुम् [ईदृशम्] निश्चयं कथम् अकरिष्यत्?"
Shri Rama said — "For how could Kaikeyi have found such resolve to wound me, if this turn of mind and this misfortune were not ordained by destiny?"

---

#### **श्लोकः १७ (Verse 17)**

श्रीरामः — "हे सौम्य (लक्ष्मण), त्वम् जानासि एव यत् न मम मातृषु कदापि भेदः आसीत्। तया अपि पूर्वम् मयि स्वपुत्रे च भेदः न कृतः।"
Shri Rama said — "You know, dear one (Lakshmana), that I never had any sense of distinction among my mothers; nor did she ever, in times past, make any difference between me and her own son."

---

#### **श्लोकः १८ (Verse 18)**

श्रीरामः — "मदभिषेकस्थगनवनवासार्थानि तस्याः कठोरोग्रवाक्यानि [दैवकारितानि] एव इति मन्ये।"
Shri Rama said — "As for those harsh and fierce words of hers—meant to stop my consecration and to drive me into exile—I can account for them by nothing other than destiny."

---

#### **श्लोकः १९ (Verse 19)**

श्रीरामः — "दैवप्रभावं विना, [स्वभावकल्याणी] [गुणान्विता] राजपुत्री कैकेयी, पतिसन्निधौ प्राकृतस्त्रीवत् मत्पीडाकरवाक्यानि कथं वदेत्?"
Shri Rama said — "If fate were not at work, how could Kaikeyi—so noble by nature, a princess endowed with such virtues—speak words that wound me, and that too in her husband's presence, like an ordinary woman?"

---

#### **श्लोकः २० (Verse 20)**

श्रीरामः — "दैवम् हि [अचिन्त्यम्], सर्वभूतेषु [अनिवार्यप्रभावयुक्तम्]। मयि तस्यां च व्यक्तम् विपर्ययः पतितः।"
Shri Rama said — "Destiny is indeed inconceivable, and its force cannot be turned back in any being. It is plain that adversity has fallen—upon me, and upon her as well."

---

#### **श्लोकः २१ (Verse 21)**

श्रीरामः — "हे सौमित्रे (सुमित्रापुत्र लक्ष्मण), [कार्यफलमात्रदृश्येन] [अप्रत्यक्षग्रहणेन] दैवेन सह कः मानवः योद्धुम् उत्सहेत?"
Shri Rama said — "O Saumitri (Lakshmana), what man can truly attempt to fight destiny—whose grip is not perceived directly, but is known only by the course and outcome of events, and nowhere else?"

---

#### **श्लोकः २२ (Verse 22)**

श्रीरामः — "सुखदुःखे, भयक्रोधौ, लाभालाभौ, भवाभवौ च—यत् किञ्चित् [एवंविधम्] भवति, तत् सर्वम् नूनम् दैवकर्म एव।"
Shri Rama said — "Happiness and sorrow, fear and anger, gain and loss, birth and death—whatever comes to be in this manner is, surely, the working of destiny."

---

#### **श्लोकः २३ (Verse 23)**

श्रीरामः — "[उग्रतपस्विनः] ऋषयः अपि [दैवपीडिताः] सन्तः, [तीव्रं] नियमं त्यक्त्वा कामक्रोधवशेन पतन्ति (भ्रश्यन्ते)।"
Shri Rama said — "Even sages of fierce austerities, when oppressed by destiny, abandon their severe disciplines and slip, overpowered by desire and anger."

---

#### **श्लोकः २४ (Verse 24)**

श्रीरामः — "यदा अत्र [अचिन्तितः] विघ्नः सहसा आगत्य [आरब्धं] कार्यं स्थगयति, तत् अपि नूनं [दैवकृतम्] एव।"
Shri Rama said — "When an unimagined obstacle suddenly arises here and brings a work already begun to a halt, that too is surely the doing of destiny."

---

#### **श्लोकः २५ (Verse 25)**

श्रीरामः — "अनया तत्त्वबुद्ध्या अहम् स्वयम् आत्मानं [स्थिरं] कृतवान् अभूवम्। मम राज्याभिषेके [असिद्धे] अपि, मम किञ्चित् अपि दुःखं नास्ति।"
Shri Rama said — "With this truth-grounded understanding I have steadied myself by my own resolve; and though my consecration has been thwarted, no grief is found in me."

---

#### **श्लोकः २६ (Verse 26)**

श्रीरामः — "तस्मात् त्वम् अपि अहम् इव [दुःखरहितः] भव, मम अनुगमनं कुरु, राज्याभिषेकसज्जताः च शीघ्रं निवर्तय।"
Shri Rama said — "Therefore, be free from grief as I am; follow my lead, and quickly withdraw the rites and arrangements intended for the coronation."

---

#### **श्लोकः २७ (Verse 27)**

श्रीरामः — "हे लक्ष्मण, अभिषेकाय [आनीतैः] एभिः एव जलघटैः, मम [भविष्यतः] तापसजीवनस्य अभिषेकः भविष्यति।"
Shri Rama said — "With these very pots of water gathered for the consecration, O Lakshmana, I shall perform the vow-bath for the life of ascetic discipline that lies ahead."

---

#### **श्लोकः २८ (Verse 28)**

श्रीरामः — "अथवा राजपात्रेषु [स्थितेन] अनेन जलेन मम किं प्रयोजनम्? मया स्वयं [आहृतं] जलम् एव मम व्रतानि पूरयिष्यति।"
Shri Rama said — "Or else, what use to me is this water kept in vessels that belong to the king? The water I draw with my own hands will serve for the observances of my vows."

---

#### **श्लोकः २९ (Verse 29)**

श्रीरामः — "हे लक्ष्मण, अस्मिन् विपर्यये शोकं मा कुरु। राज्यात् वनवासः एव [श्रेयान्]।"
Shri Rama said — "Do not grieve, O Lakshmana, at this reversal of fortune. Whether it is kingship or forest-dwelling—forest-dwelling is the greater glory."

---

#### **श्लोकः ३० (Verse 30)**

श्रीरामः — "हे लक्ष्मण, अस्मिन् कार्यविघ्ने अस्माकं कनिष्ठमाता अधिकं न शङ्कनीया। दैववशेन सा [अप्रियं] वदति, दैवबलं कीदृशं महत् अस्ति इति त्वं जानासि।"
Shri Rama said — "O Lakshmana, in this obstruction to our intended act, our younger mother should not be excessively suspected. Overpowered by destiny she speaks what is unpleasant—you know how strong the force of destiny can be."
