**English:**

**Sanskrit:**

**English:**
> Sumantra – "O Queen! When you have abandoned your own husband, King Dasharatha, who is the lord of the entire animate and inanimate world, then no evil deed is left impossible for you to commit. I indeed consider that you are not only the slayer of your husband, but ultimately also the destroyer of your dynasty."

### **2.35.7**

**Sanskrit:**

**English:**
> Sumantra – "Alas! You are tormenting with your actions even King Dasharatha, who is invincible like Indra (the king of gods), unshakeable like a mountain, and undisturbed like the ocean."

### **2.35.8**

**Sanskrit:**

**English:**
> Sumantra – "King Dasharatha is your husband, protector, and granter of boons. Do not disrespect him. For women, the wish of a husband is of greater importance than even ten million sons."

### **2.35.9**

**Sanskrit:**
> सुमन्त्रः - «[अस्मिन्] कुले राज्ञि [परलोकङ्गते] सति, पुत्राणाम् वयः विचिन्त्य, यः [ज्येष्ठः] पुत्रः भवति, सः एव राज्यम् प्राप्नोति। [इक्ष्वाकुवंशस्य] [स्वामिनि] [महाराजे] [दशरथे] [जीवति] एव, त्वम् राजकुलस्य [इमम्] [परम्परागतम्] आचारम् विनाशयितुम् इच्छसि।»

**English:**
> Sumantra – "In this dynasty, after the passing away of the king, considering the age of the sons, it is the eldest son who attains the kingdom. You wish to destroy this traditional custom of the royal dynasty even while King Dasharatha, the lord of the Ikshvaku race, is still alive."

### **2.35.10**

**Sanskrit:**
> सुमन्त्रः - «तव पुत्रः भरतः राजा भवतु, [इमाम्] पृथिवीम् च पालयतु। किन्तु वयम् तु तत्र एव गमिष्यामः, यत्र श्रीरामः गमिष्यति।»

**English:**
> Sumantra – "Let your son Bharata become the king and rule over this earth. But as for us, we will go only to that place where Shri Rama goes."

### **2.35.11**

**Sanskrit:**
> सुमन्त्रः - «तव राज्ये [कश्चिदपि] ब्राह्मणः निवासम् न करिष्यति। यदि त्वम् अद्य [तादृशम्] [मर्यादाहीनम्] कर्म करिष्यसि, तर्हि नूनम् वयम् [सर्वे] [तम्] एव मार्गम् गमिष्यामः, यः श्रीरामेण [अनुसृतः]।»

**English:**
> Sumantra – "No Brahmana will reside in your kingdom. If you commit such an unrighteous act today, then certainly all of us will proceed on that very path which has been followed by Shri Rama."

### **2.35.12–13**

**Sanskrit:**

**English:**
> Sumantra – "All your relatives and virtuous Brahmanas will also abandon you. O Queen! Then, having obtained this kingdom, what joy will you receive? Alas! You wish to commit such an unrighteous act."

### **2.35.14**

**Sanskrit:**

**English:**

### **2.35.15**

**Sanskrit:**

**English:**
> Sumantra – "Or why do the reproachful, terrifying, and burning curses of great Brahmarshis not utterly destroy a stone-hearted woman like you, who stands ready to banish Shri Rama from home?"

### **2.35.16**

**Sanskrit:**

**English:**

### **2.35.17**

**Sanskrit:**
> सुमन्त्रः - «कैकेयि! अहम् मन्ये यत् तव मातुः [स्वकुलानुरूपः] [यादृशः] स्वभावः अभूत्, [तादृशः] एव तव अपि अस्ति। लोके [कथिता] [इयम्] लोकोक्तिः [सत्या] एव यत् निम्बात् मधु न स्रवति।»

**English:**
> Sumantra – "O Kaikeyi! I consider that just as your mother had a disposition conforming to her lineage, such is yours as well. This proverb spoken in the world is indeed true: honey never drips from a neem tree."

### **2.35.18**

**Sanskrit:**

**English:**
> Sumantra – "We also know about your mother's obstinacy. What was heard earlier regarding this matter is now being told. Once, a boon-giving ascetic granted a most excellent boon to your father."

**English:**
> Sumantra – "By the power of that boon, the King of Kekaya began to understand the speech of all creatures. Even the sounds of creatures belonging to the animal kingdom were understood by him."

**Sanskrit:**

**English:**
> Sumantra – "One day, your highly radiant father was lying on his bed. At that very moment, the cry of a bird named Jrimbha fell upon his ears. The meaning of its speech was understood by him. Therefore, he laughed there repeatedly."

### **2.35.21**

**Sanskrit:**
> सुमन्त्रः - «[तस्याम्] एव शय्यायाम् तव माता अपि [सुप्ता] अभूत्। 'राजा माम् एव उपहसति' इति [विचिन्त्य], सा [कुपिता] [सञ्जाता]। कण्ठे मृत्युपाशम् आबन्द्धुम् [इच्छन्ती] सा अवादीत् - 'सौम्य! नरेश्वर! तव हासस्य [किम्] कारणम् अस्ति, [एतत्] अहम् ज्ञातुम् इच्छामि।'»

**English:**
> Sumantra – "Your mother was also sleeping on that very bed. Thinking that the king was mocking her, she became enraged, and desiring to tie a noose of death around her neck, she said—'O gentle one! O lord of men! What is the reason for your laughter? I wish to know this.'"

### **2.35.22**

**Sanskrit:**
> सुमन्त्रः - «तदा राज्ञा [सा] देवी [उक्ता] - 'राज्ञि! यदि अहम् [स्वकीयस्य] हासस्य कारणम् कथयामि, तर्हि [तस्मिन्] एव क्षणे मम मृत्युः भविष्यति। अत्र सन्देहः नास्ति।'»

**English:**
> Sumantra – "Then the king said to that queen—'O Queen! If I disclose the reason for my laughter, at that very moment my death will occur. There is no doubt about this.'"

### **2.35.23**

**Sanskrit:**

**English:**
> Sumantra – "O Queen! Hearing this, your queen-mother said again to your father, the King of Kekaya—'Whether you live or die, tell me the reason. In the future, you will not be able to mock me again.'"

### **2.35.24**

**Sanskrit:**

**English:**
> Sumantra – "When his beloved queen spoke thus, the King of Kekaya went to that boon-giving ascetic and related the whole matter truthfully."

### **2.35.25**

**Sanskrit:**

**English:**

### **2.35.27**

**Sanskrit:**
> सुमन्त्रः - «त्वम् अपि एवम् दुर्जनमार्गे [स्थिता] पापे एव दृष्टिम् [निधाय] मोहवशात् राजानम् प्रति [इमम्] [अनुचितम्] आग्रहम् करोषि।»

**English:**
> Sumantra – "In this very manner, you too, established on the path of the wicked, keeping your gaze fixed on sin alone, are making this improper insistence to the king out of delusion."

### **2.35.28**

**Sanskrit:**
> सुमन्त्रः - «अद्य मह्यम् [इयम्] लोकोक्तिः [सर्वथा] [सत्या] प्रतिभाति यत् पुत्राः [पितृसमाः] भवन्ति, कन्याः च [मातृसमाः] भवन्ति।»

**English:**
> Sumantra – "Today, this popular saying appears entirely true to me: that sons resemble their fathers, and daughters resemble their mothers."

### **2.35.29**

**Sanskrit:**

**English:**
> Sumantra – "Do not become like this—do not make this proverb come true in your life. Accept whatever the king has said. Following the wish of your husband, become one who grants refuge to this populace here."

### **2.35.30**

**Sanskrit:**

**English:**
> Sumantra – "Being misled by the words of people who possess sinful thoughts, do not engage your husband and protector of the world, who is radiant like Indra (the king of gods), in an improper action."

---

### **2.35.31**

**Sanskrit:**

**English:**
> Sumantra – "O Queen! The lotus-eyed, illustrious King Dasharatha remains distant from sin. He will not make his promise false."

---

### **2.35.32**

**Sanskrit:**
> सुमन्त्रः - «श्रीरामचन्द्रः भ्रातृषु [ज्येष्ठः], [उदारः], [कर्मठः], [स्वधर्मपालकः], [जीवलोकरक्षकः], [बलवान्] च अस्ति। [अस्य] [अस्मिन्] राज्ये अभिषेकः भवतु।»

**English:**
> Sumantra – "Shri Ramachandra is the eldest among his brothers, generous, diligent, an observer of his own duty, protector of the living world, and mighty. Let his consecration take place over this kingdom."

---

### **2.35.33**

**Sanskrit:**
> सुमन्त्रः - «देवि! यदि श्रीरामः [स्वकीयम्] पितरम् राजानम् दशरथम् [त्यक्त्वा] वनम् गमिष्यति, तर्हि लोके तव [महती] निन्दा भविष्यति।»

**English:**
> Sumantra – "O Queen! If Shri Rama leaves his father, King Dasharatha, and goes to the forest, there will be great reproach of you in the world."

---

### **2.35.34**

**Sanskrit:**
> सुमन्त्रः - «अतः श्रीरामचन्द्रः एव [स्वकीयम्] राज्यम् पालयतु, त्वम् च [निश्चिन्ता] तिष्ठ। श्रीरामम् विना [अन्यः] [कश्चिदपि] राजा [अस्मिन्] [श्रेष्ठे] नगरे [स्थित्वा] तव [अनुकूलम्] आचरणम् कर्तुम् न शक्नोति।»

**English:**
> Sumantra – "Therefore, let Shri Ramachandra alone rule his kingdom, and you remain carefree. No other king residing in this excellent city can act favorably towards you except Shri Rama."

---

### **2.35.35**

**Sanskrit:**
> सुमन्त्रः - «श्रीरामे युवराजपदे [प्रतिष्ठिते] सति, [महाधनुर्धरः] राजा दशरथः [पूर्वजानाम्] वृत्तान्तम् [स्मृत्वा] स्वयम् वनम् प्रवेक्ष्यति।»

**English:**
> Sumantra – "After Shri Rama is consecrated in the office of the crown prince, the great archer King Dasharatha, remembering the conduct of his ancestors, will himself enter the forest."

### **2.35.36–37**

**Sanskrit:**
> एवम् सुमन्त्रेण [कृताञ्जलिना] [तस्मिन्] राजभवने [सान्त्वनापूर्णैः] [तीक्ष्णैः] च वचनैः कैकेयीम् [विचालयितुम्] चेष्टा [कृता]। किन्तु सा [किञ्चिदपि] न [चलिता]। देव्याः कैकेय्याः मनसि न तु क्षोभः [सञ्जातः], न च दुःखम् [अभूत्]। [तस्मिन्] समये तस्याः मुखवर्णे [कश्चिदपि] भेदः न [दृष्टः]।

**English:**
> In this manner, joining his hands, Sumantra repeatedly attempted to dissuade Kaikeyi in that royal palace with soothing as well as sharp words, but she did not budge in the least. In the mind of Queen Kaikeyi, there was neither agitation nor grief. At that time, no change was seen even in the complexion of her face.
