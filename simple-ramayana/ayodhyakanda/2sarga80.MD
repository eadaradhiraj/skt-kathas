ततः उच्चां निम्नां सजलां निर्जलां च भूमिं जानन्तः, शिबिर-निर्माणाय सूत्र-कर्मणि कुशलाः, मार्ग-रक्षणादौ स्व-कर्मणि सदा सावधानाः शूराः च प्रस्थिताः। तथा च भूमि-खनकाः, सुरङ्ग-निर्मातारः, नद्यादीनां तरणाय शीघ्रं साधनानि उपस्थापयन्तः वा जल-प्रवाहस्य निरोधं कुर्वन्तः वा वेतन-भोगिनः शिल्पिनः अपि प्रस्थिताः। एतैः सह भवन-निर्मातारः, रथ-यन्त्रादीनां निर्मातारः, काष्ठ-तक्षकाः, मार्ग-रक्षकाः, वृक्ष-च्छेदकाः पाचकाः च प्रस्थिताः। अपि च सुधा-लेपनादि-कर्मकराः, वंशेन कटादि-शूर्पादि-निर्मातारः, चर्मणा पर्याणादि-कर्तारः, मार्गस्य विशेष-ज्ञानिनः समर्थाः पुरुषाः च प्रथमं प्रस्थिताः ॥ 1-3 ॥

**Translation:** Then, brave men knowing the high, low, watery, and waterless terrains, skilled in the use of measuring strings for camp construction, and always attentive in their duties like guarding the paths, set out. Along with them, earth-diggers, tunnel-makers, and salaried artisans who quickly provided means to cross rivers or obstructed water flows, also set out. With them set out building constructors, makers of chariots and machines, wood-carvers, path-guards, tree-cutters, and cooks. Furthermore, workers for whitewashing, makers of bamboo mats and winnowing baskets, makers of leather saddles, and capable men with special knowledge of the paths set out first.

तस्मिन् समये मार्गं समीकर्तुम् एकः विशालः जन-समूहः महता हर्षेण वन-प्रदेशं प्रति अग्रे गतः। सः जन-समूहः पूर्णिमा-दिने प्रवृद्धस्य समुद्रस्य महा-वेगः इव शोभितः ॥ 4 ॥

**Translation:** At that time, a huge group of people went forward towards the forest region with great joy to level the path. That crowd looked splendid like the great tide of the ocean swelling on a full-moon day.

ते मार्ग-निर्माणे निपुणाः शिल्पिनः स्व-स्व-दल-सहिताः अनेक-विधैः उपकरणैः सह अग्रे प्रस्थिताः ॥ 5 ॥

**Translation:** Those artisans, skilled in road construction, set out forward with their respective teams and various types of tools.

तैः जनैः मार्गे स्थिताः लताः, वल्ल्यः, गुल्माः, स्थाणवः, पाषाणाः, नाना-विधाः वृक्षाः च छिन्नाः अपसारिताः च। एवं ते अग्रे मार्ग-निर्माणं कुर्वन्तः गताः ॥ 6 ॥

**Translation:** By those people, the creepers, vines, bushes, stumps, stones, and various kinds of trees located on the path were cut and removed. Thus, they went forward building the road.

प्रदेशेषु वृक्ष-हीनेषु कैश्चित् जनैः वृक्षाः रोपिताः। कैश्चित् कुठारैः, टङ्कैः, दात्रैः च क्वचित् क्वचित् मार्गे स्थिताः गुल्माः छिन्नाः। अन्यैः जनैः बल-पूर्वकं वीरण-स्तम्बाः उन्मूलिताः। तथा च अन्यत्र उन्मूलिताः वा छिन्नाः वा वृक्षाः तैः विभिन्न-स्थानानि गत्वा तत्र रोपिताः। विशालाः वृक्षाः पूर्वतः एव दृढं बद्ध-मूलाः, पुष्टाः, विस्तृताः सर्वे अपि तैः छिन्नाः ॥ 7-9 ॥

**Translation:** In regions devoid of trees, some people planted trees. Some cut the bushes situated here and there on the path with axes, chisels, and sickles. Other people forcefully uprooted tufts of virana grass. And trees uprooted or cut elsewhere were taken to various places by them and planted there. Even large trees that were firmly rooted from before, strong, and expansive were all cut down by them.

तैः नरैः सेतु-बन्धन-योग्यस्य जलस्य उपरि सेतवः बद्धाः। दृष्टा कर्कर-युक्ता भूमिः ताडयित्वा मृदू-कृता। यत्र च जल-प्रवाहाय मार्ग-निर्माणम् आवश्यकं मतम्, तत्र बन्धः छिन्नः। एवं विभिन्न-देशेषु आवश्यकताम् अनुसृत्य तैः कार्यं कृतम् ॥ 10 ॥

**Translation:** Those men built bridges over water suitable for bridging. The earth seen containing gravel was beaten and made soft. And where the construction of a path was deemed necessary for the flow of water, the embankment was breached. Thus, work was done by them in various regions according to necessity.

तैः अल्प-कालेन एव बहु-जल-पूर्णाः विशालाः तडागाः कुल्याः च निर्मिताः। ते नाना-विधैः आकारैः युक्ताः, समुद्र-समानाः च प्रतीताः ॥ 11 ॥

**Translation:** In a short time, large ponds and canals full of abundant water were constructed by them. They were of various shapes and appeared like the ocean.

जल-हीनेषु स्थानेषु तैः जनैः अनेके कूपाः खाताः। तेषां कूपानां समीपे अनेकाः वेदिकाः अपि निर्मिताः। जलेन पूर्णाः ते कूपाः अतीव सुन्दराः दृष्टाः। एवं तैः शिल्पिभिः मार्गः विशालैः वस्त्रैः सज्जितः, पताकाभिः च शोभितः। अपि च सः मार्गः समी-कृतः, उत्तम-चन्दनादिभिः सिक्तः, विविधैः पुष्पैः च अलङ्कृतः ॥ 12-13 ॥

**Translation:** In waterless places, many wells were dug by those men. Near those wells, many platforms were also built. Those wells full of water looked very beautiful. Thus, the path was decorated with large cloths and adorned with flags by those artisans. Furthermore, that path was leveled, sprinkled with excellent sandalwood water, and adorned with various flowers.

एवं सः सेना-मार्गः देव-मार्गः इव अतीव शोभितः। तत्र सुधा-चूर्णम्, इष्टका-चूर्णं, क्षुद्र-पाषाणान् च आस्तीर्य, सा भूमिः कुट्टयित्वा दृढी-कृता। तस्य मार्गस्य पार्श्वयोः पुष्पैः शोभिताः वृक्षाः रोपिताः। तेषु वृक्षेषु मत्तैः पक्षिभिः कलरवः कृतः ॥ 14 ॥

**Translation:** Thus, that army path looked extremely splendid like the path of the gods. Having spread lime powder, brick powder, and small stones there, that earth was pounded and made firm. Trees adorned with flowers were planted on both sides of that path. Intoxicated birds made sweet sounds in those trees.

मार्गे निर्मिते सति, तत्र तत्र शिबिरादि-निर्माणाय येभ्यः अधिकारः दत्तः, ते कार्ये दत्त-चित्ताः स्थिताः। तैः भरतस्य आज्ञाम् अनुसृत्य सेवकाः कार्यार्थम् आदिष्टाः। येषु सुन्दरेषु प्रदेशेषु स्वादु-फलानि अधिकानि स्थितानि, तत्र तैः शिबिराणि कारितानि। अपि च भरतस्य अभीष्टानि मार्ग-भूषण-रूपाणि तानि शिबिराणि तैः नाना-विधैः अलङ्कारैः अधिकं सज्जितानि ॥ 15-16 ॥

**Translation:** Once the path was built, those who were given authority to construct camps here and there remained focused on their tasks. Following Bharata's order, servants were instructed for the work by them. In those beautiful regions where sweet fruits were abundant, camps were caused to be built by them. Moreover, those camps, which were Bharata's desired ornaments of the path, were further decorated by them with various kinds of adornments.

महात्मनः भरतस्य वासाय स्थानानां निर्मितानां प्रतिष्ठा वास्तु-कर्म-ज्ञैः विद्वद्भिः उत्तमेषु नक्षत्रेषु मुहूर्तेषु च कारिता ॥ 17 ॥

**Translation:** The consecration of the constructed dwellings for the residence of the great-souled Bharata was done by learned experts in architecture during excellent asterisms and auspicious moments.

मार्गे निर्मितानि तानि विश्राम-स्थानानि इन्द्र-पुरी इव शोभितानि। तानि परितः परिखाः खाताः, धूलि-मृत्तिकानाम् उच्चाः राशयः च कृताः। शिबिराणाम् अन्तः इन्द्रनील-मणि-निर्मिताः प्रतिमाः सज्जिताः। वीथिभिः मार्गैः च तानि विशेषतः शोभितानि। राज-गृहैः देव-स्थानैः च युक्तानि तानि शिबिराणि सुधा-लिप्तैः प्राकारैः परिवृतानि। सर्वाणि विश्राम-स्थानानि पताकाभिः सुशोभितानि। सर्वत्र विशालानां मार्गाणां सुन्दर-रीत्या निर्माणं कृतम्। कपोत-गृहैः उच्चैः श्रेष्ठैः विमानैः च तेषां सर्वेषां शिबिराणां महती शोभा जाता ॥ 18-20 ॥

**Translation:** Those resting places built on the path looked splendid like Indra's city. Moats were dug around them, and high mounds of dust and dirt were made. Inside the camps, statues made of sapphire were arranged. They were specially beautified with avenues and paths. Endowed with royal houses and temples, those camps were surrounded by whitewashed enclosure walls. All resting places were beautifully adorned with flags. Everywhere, large roads were constructed in a beautiful manner. A great splendor came to all those camps with dovecotes and high, excellent palaces.

गङ्गा नाना-विधैः वृक्षैः वनैः च सुशोभिता, शीतल-निर्मल-जलेन पूर्णा, महा-मत्स्यैः व्याप्ता च स्थिता। तस्याः गङ्गायाः तीरं यावत् निर्मितः सः रमणीयः राज-मार्गः तस्मिन् समये अतीव शोभितः। उत्तमैः शिल्पिभिः तस्य निर्माणं कृतम्। रात्रि-काले सः मार्गः चन्द्रेण तारा-गणैः च मण्डितः निर्मलः आकाशः इव सुशोभितः ॥ 21-22 ॥

**Translation:** The Ganga stood beautifully adorned with various kinds of trees and forests, full of cool and clear water, and pervaded with large fish. That charming royal road built up to the bank of that Ganga looked very splendid at that time. Its construction was done by excellent artisans. At nighttime, that path adorned with the moon and clusters of stars looked as splendid as the clear sky.
