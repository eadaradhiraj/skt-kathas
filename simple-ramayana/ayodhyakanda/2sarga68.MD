### Verse 1

**Sanskrit:**

[मार्कण्डेय-आदि-उक्तं] [तत्] वचनं महर्षिणा वसिष्ठेन [श्रुतम्] । ततः [तेन] [सर्वान्] मित्र-मन्त्रि-ब्राह्मणान् प्रति [इदं] वचनम् [उक्तम्] ॥

**English:**

Hearing such words spoken by Markandeya and the others, the great sage Vashistha replied thus to all his friends, ministers, and the Brahmins:

---

### Verse 2

**Sanskrit:**

वसिष्ठः - राज्ञा दशरथेन भरताय राज्यं [दत्तम्] । [सः] भ्रात्रा शत्रुघ्नेन सह मातुल-गृहे [परम-सुखेन] [प्रसन्न-मनसा] निवसति ॥

**English:**

Vashistha - "King Dasharatha bestowed the kingdom upon Bharata. He now resides most happily and joyfully at his maternal uncle's home together with his brother Shatrughna."

---

### Verse 3

**Sanskrit:**

वसिष्ठः - [तौ] [वीरौ] भ्रातरौ आनेतुं [शीघ्र-गामिनः] [अश्व-आरूढाः] दूताः इतः शीघ्रं गच्छन्तु । [अस्माभिः] [एतस्मात्] [अन्यत्] [किं] विचार्यते ॥

**English:**

Vashistha - "To bring those two brave brothers, swift horse-mounted messengers should depart from here at once. What else can we possibly consider?"

---

### Verse 4

**Sanskrit:**

ततः [सर्वैः] वसिष्ठाय [एतत्] [उक्तम्] यत् दूताः अवश्यम् प्रेष्यन्ताम् । [तेषां] [तत्] वचनं श्रुत्वा वसिष्ठेन दूतान् सम्बोध्य [इदम्] [उक्तम्] ॥

**English:**

Thereupon, all said to Vashistha, "Yes, messengers must certainly be dispatched." Hearing those words of theirs, Vashistha addressed the messengers and said:

---

### Verse 5

**Sanskrit:**

वसिष्ठः - हे सिद्धार्थ हे विजय हे जयन्त हे अशोक हे नन्दन [यूयम्] अत्र आगच्छत । [युष्माभिः] [कर्तव्यं] कार्यं शृणुत [यत्] [अहम्] [युष्मान्] [सर्वान्] कथयामि ॥

**English:**

Vashistha - "O Siddhartha, Vijaya, Jayanta, Ashoka, and Nandana, all of you come here. Listen to the duty to be carried out, which I impart to you all."

---

### Verse 6

**Sanskrit:**

वसिष्ठः - [यूयं] [शीघ्र-गामिषु] अश्वेषु [आरूढाः] राज-गृह-नगरं शीघ्रं गच्छत । शोक-भावम् [अप्रकटयन्तः] [मम] आज्ञानुसारं भरताय [एवम्] वदत ॥

**English:**

Vashistha - "Mounting swift horses, go immediately to the city of Rajagriha. And without revealing any grief, speak thus to Bharata according to my command:"

---

### Verse 7

**Sanskrit:**

वसिष्ठः - हे कुमार पुरोहितेन [सर्वैः] मन्त्रिभिः [भवतः] कुशलम् [उक्तम्] । अधुना [भवान्] इतः शीघ्रं चलतु यतः अयोध्यायां [भवतः] [अतीव-आवश्यकं] कार्यं वर्तते ॥

**English:**

Vashistha - "'O Prince, the family priest and all the ministers convey their greetings of welfare to you. Depart from here at once, for an extremely urgent matter awaits you in Ayodhya.'"

---

### Verse 8

**Sanskrit:**

वसिष्ठः - भरताय श्री-राम-चन्द्रस्य वन-वासं पितुः मरणं मा वदत । [एताभिः] परिस्थितिभिः रघु-कुले [उत्पन्नस्य] [महतः] हाहाकारस्य चर्चां मा कुरुत ॥

**English:**

Vashistha - "Do not reveal to Bharata the forest exile of Shri Ramachandra nor the passing of his father. And do not even mention the great lamentation that has erupted among the descendants of Raghu due to these circumstances."

---

### Verse 9

**Sanskrit:**

वसिष्ठः - केकय-राज-भरतयोः उपहारार्थं [कौशेयानि] वस्त्राणि [उत्तमानि] आभूषणानि गृह्णीत । ततः [यूयम्] इतः शीघ्रं चलत ॥

**English:**

Vashistha - "Take silk garments and precious ornaments as presents for the King of Kekaya and for Bharata, and then set forth from here without delay."

---

### Verse 10

**Sanskrit:**

केकय-देशं [गन्तु-कामैः] [तैः] दूतैः मार्ग-व्यय-धनं [गृहीतम्] । ततः [ते] [उत्तमेषु] अश्वेषु [आरूढाः] स्व-स्व-गृहं [गताः] ॥

**English:**

The messengers intending to travel to Kekaya took their travel allowances. Then, mounting noble horses, they went to their respective homes.

---

### Verse 11

**Sanskrit:**

ततः [तैः] [शेषां] यात्रा-सज्जां समाप्य वसिष्ठस्य आज्ञा [प्राप्ता] । ततः [सर्वे] दूताः [तस्मात्] स्थानात् शीघ्रं [प्रस्थिताः] ॥

**English:**

Completing the remaining preparations for the journey, they received Vashistha's permission. Thereupon, all the messengers promptly set out from that place.

---

### Verse 12

**Sanskrit:**

अपर-तालस्य दक्षिण-भागे प्रलम्ब-गिरेः उत्तर-भागे [प्रवहन्त्याः] मालिनी-नद्याः तटं दूतैः [प्राप्तम्] । ततः [ते] [तस्याः] नद्याः तटेन अग्रे [गताः] ॥

**English:**

The messengers reached the bank of the Malini River, which flows between the southern side of Mount Aparatala and the northern side of Mount Pralambagiri. Then they proceeded forward along the riverbank.

---

### Verse 13

**Sanskrit:**

हस्तिनापुरे [तैः] गङ्गा [तीर्णा] ततः [ते] पश्चिम-दिशं [गताः] । [तैः] पाञ्चाल-देशः [प्राप्तः] ततः [ते] कुरु-जाङ्गल-प्रदेशस्य मध्येन अग्रे [गताः] ॥

**English:**

They crossed the Ganga at Hastinapura and rode westward. Having arrived in the land of Panchala, they continued forward straight through the heart of Kurujangala.

---

### Verse 14

**Sanskrit:**

मार्गे [सुन्दर-पुष्प-सुशोभितानि] सरोवराणि [निर्मल-जलाः] नद्यः दूतैः [दृष्टाः] । ततः [ते] दूताः कार्य-वशात् तीव्र-गत्या अग्रे [गताः] ॥

**English:**

Along the way, the messengers beheld lakes adorned with beautiful blossoms and streams with pristine water. Urged on by their mission, they galloped forward at high speed.

---

### Verse 15

**Sanskrit:**

ततः [स्वच्छ-जल-सुशोभितायाः] [जल-पूर्णायाः] [नाना-पक्षि-सेवितायाः] [दिव्यायाः] शरदण्डा-नद्याः तटं दूतैः [प्राप्तम्] । ततः [ते] [तां] नदीं वेगेन [उत्तीर्णाः] ॥

**English:**

Next, the messengers reached the bank of the sacred Sharadanda River, adorned with pure water, brimming full, and frequented by diverse birds. Then they crossed that river with great speed.

---

### Verse 16

**Sanskrit:**

शरदण्डा-नद्याः पश्चिम-तटे [सत्योपयाचन-नामकः] [एकः] [दिव्यः] वृक्षः [स्थितः] । [तस्य] [वन्दनीयस्य] वृक्षस्य समीपं गत्वा दूतैः प्रदक्षिणा [कृता] । ततः अग्रे गत्वा [तैः] कुलिङ्ग-पुर्यां प्रवेशः [कृतः] ॥

**English:**

On the western bank of the Sharadanda stood a divine tree named Satyopayachana, where prayers were unfailingly granted. Approaching that venerable tree, the messengers circumambulated it reverently, and proceeding onward, they entered the city of Kulinga.

---

### Verse 17

**Sanskrit:**

ततः तेजोऽभिभवन-ग्रामं तीर्त्वा [ते] अभिकाल-ग्रामं [प्राप्ताः] । ततः अग्रे गत्वा [तैः] राज्ञः दशरथस्य [पितृ-पितामह-सेविता] [पुण्य-सलिला] इक्षुमती-नदी [तीर्णा] ॥

**English:**

Passing through the village of Tejobhibhavana, they reached the village of Abhikala. Proceeding further, they crossed the holy river Ikshumati, which had been revered by King Dasharatha's ancestors.

---

### Verse 18

**Sanskrit:**

तत्र केवलम् [अञ्जलि-परिमितं] जलं पीत्वा तपस्यां [कुर्वन्तः] [वेद-पारगामिनः] ब्राह्मणाः दूतैः [दृष्टाः] । ततः [ते] दूताः बाह्लीक-देशस्य मध्य-भागे [स्थितस्य] [सुदामा-नामकस्य] पर्वतस्य समीपं [प्राप्ताः] ॥

**English:**

There, the messengers saw Brahmins learned in the Vedas who performed austerities drinking only a handful of water. Then the messengers arrived near Mount Sudama in the heart of the Bahlika country.

---

### Verses 19–20

**Sanskrit:**

[तस्य] पर्वतस्य शिखरे [स्थितस्य] [भगवतः] विष्णोः चरण-चिह्नं दूतैः [दृष्टम्] । ततः [ते] विपाशा-नद्याः [तट-वर्तिनः] शाल्मली-वृक्षस्य समीपं [गताः] । ततः [बह्वीः] नदीः सरांसि [सिंह-व्याघ्र-प्रमुखान्] वन-जन्तून् [पश्यन्तः] [ते] [विशालेन] मार्गेण अग्रे [गताः] । [ते] स्व-स्वामिनः आज्ञां शीघ्रं पालयितुम् [इच्छुकाः] [जाताः] ॥

**English:**

After beholding the footprint of Lord Vishnu on the mountain peak, they approached the Vipasha River and the Shalmali tree on its bank. Riding past many streams, lakes, and wild beasts like lions and tigers, they advanced along the broad highway, eager to swiftly carry out their master's command.

---

### Verse 21

**Sanskrit:**

[तेषां] दूतानाम् अश्वाः [श्रान्ताः] [जाताः] । [सः] मार्गः [अति-दीर्घः] अपि [उपद्रव-रहितः] [जातः] । [तं] मार्गं तीर्त्वा [सर्वे] दूताः [कष्ट-रहितम्] [श्रेष्ठ-नगरं] गिरि-व्रजं शीघ्रं [प्राप्ताः] ॥

**English:**

The horses of the messengers grew weary from the journey. Though the route was long, it was peaceful and free of peril. Having traversed it, all the messengers arrived swiftly and effortlessly at the noble city of Girivraja.

---

### Verse 22

**Sanskrit:**

[ते] दूताः स्व-स्वामिनः वसिष्ठस्य प्रियं कर्तुं प्रजा-वर्गस्य रक्षां कर्तुं महाराज-दशरथस्य [वंश-परम्परा-गतं] राज्यं भरतेन [स्वी-कृतं] कारयितुं [सादर-तत्-पराः] [जाताः] । ततः [ते] [महत्या] त्वरया चलित्वा रात्रौ एव [तस्मिन्] नगरे [प्रविष्टाः] ॥

**English:**

Devoted to pleasing their master Vashistha, safeguarding the people, and having Bharata accept King Dasharatha's ancestral realm, the messengers rode with great haste and entered that city during the night itself.
