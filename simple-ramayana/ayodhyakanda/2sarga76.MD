1. एवम् [शोक-सन्तप्तः] [कैकयी-कुमारः] भरतः [वक्तृ-श्रेष्ठेन] महर्षि-वसिष्ठेन [उत्तमया] वाण्या [उक्तः]।
**Translation:** Thus, Bharata, the grief-stricken son of Kaikeyi, was addressed by the great sage Vashistha, the best among speakers, with excellent words.
2. वसिष्ठः - हे [महा-यशस्विन्] राज-कुमार तव कल्याणम् अस्तु। [एतं] शोकं त्यज यतः अनेन किमपि न सिद्ध्यति। इदानीं [समयोचिते] कर्तव्ये ध्यानं देहि। राज-दशरथस्य शवं दाह-संस्काराय नेतुम् [उत्तमः] प्रबन्धः क्रियताम्।
**Translation:** Vashistha: "O highly illustrious prince, may there be well-being for you. Abandon this grief because nothing is accomplished by it. Now pay attention to the duty appropriate for this time. Let an excellent arrangement be made to take King Dasharatha's body for the cremation rites."
3. महर्षि-वसिष्ठस्य वचनं [श्रुत्वा] [धर्मज्ञेन] भरतेन भूमौ [पतित्वा] तस्मै [स-अष्टाङ्गः] प्रणामः [कृतः]। मन्त्रिभिः पितुः [सम्पूर्णस्य] प्रेत-कर्मणः प्रबन्धः [कारितः]।
**Translation:** Having heard the words of the great sage Vashistha, the righteous Bharata, falling to the ground, offered an eight-limbed prostration to him. By the ministers, the arrangement of the entire funeral rite of the father was caused to be made.
4. राज-दशरथस्य शवः तैल-कटाहात् बहिः [आनीय] भूमौ [स्थापितः]। तैले [दीर्घ-काल-मग्नत्वात्] तस्य मुखं [किञ्चित्-पीत-वर्णं] [जातम्]। तं [दृष्ट्वा] राजा दशरथः स्वपिति इति [प्रतीतम्]।
**Translation:** King Dasharatha's body, having been brought out from the cauldron of oil, was placed on the ground. Due to being submerged in oil for a long time, his face had become slightly yellowish in color. Having seen him, it appeared as if King Dasharatha was sleeping.
5. तदनन्तरं [मृतः] राजा दशरथः [सु-प्रक्षालितः] [नाना-रत्न-विभूषितायाम्] [उत्तमायां] शय्यायां [शायितः]। ततः तस्य पुत्रेण भरतेन [अतीव-दुःखितेन] विलापः [आरब्धः]।
**Translation:** Afterwards, the deceased King Dasharatha, well-washed, was laid upon an excellent bed adorned with various jewels. Then, his extremely sorrowful son Bharata began his lamentation.
6. भरतः - हे राजन् पर-देशे [स्थितः] अहं भवतः समीपं न [आगतः]। तावत् एव [धर्मज्ञं] श्री-रामं [महा-बलं] लक्ष्मणं वनं [प्रेष्य] भवता एवं स्वर्गं गन्तुं निश्चयः कथं [कृतः]।
*(Corrected: प्रेषयित्वा → प्रेष्य)*
**Translation:** Bharata: "O King, staying in a foreign land, I did not come near you. At that very time, having sent the righteous Sri Rama and the mighty Lakshmana to the forest, how was the decision made by you to thus go to heaven?"
7. भरतः - हे महा-राज [अनायास-कृत-महा-कर्मणा] [पुरुष-सिंहेन] श्री-रामेण [हीनम्] [एतं] [दुःखितं] सेवकं [त्यक्त्वा] भवान् कुत्र गमिष्यति।
**Translation:** Bharata: "O Great King, having abandoned this sorrowful servant, deprived of Sri Rama, the lion among men who performs great deeds effortlessly, where will you go?"
8. भरतः - हे तात भवान् स्वर्गं [गतः]। श्री-रामेण वनस्य आश्रयः [गृहीतः]। [एतस्यां] दशायां भवतः [एतस्मिन्] नगरे प्रजानां योग-क्षेमस्य [निश्चितां] व्यवस्थां कः करिष्यति।
*(Corrected: निश्चिन्ताम् → निश्चिताम्)*
**Translation:** Bharata: "O father, you have gone to heaven. Sri Rama has taken refuge in the forest. In this situation, who will make a secure arrangement for the welfare and protection of the subjects in this city of yours?"
9. भरतः - हे राजन् भवता विना [विधवा-समाना] [जाता] [एषा] पृथ्वी न शोभते। [एषा] पुरी अपि मम [चन्द्र-हीन-रात्रि-समाना] [श्री-हीना] प्रतीयते।
**Translation:** Bharata: "O King, without you, this earth, having become like a widow, does not shine. To me, this city also appears devoid of splendor, like a night without the moon."
10. एवं [दीन-चित्तः] विलापं [कुर्वन्] भरतः महा-मुनि-वसिष्ठेन पुनः [उक्तः]।
**Translation:** Bharata, lamenting thus with a distressed mind, was addressed again by the great sage Vashistha.
11. वसिष्ठः - हे [महा-बाहो] [एतस्य] महा-राजस्य कृते यानि प्रेत-कर्माणि [कर्तव्यानि] तानि [अविचार्य] [शान्त-चित्तः] कुरु।
**Translation:** Vashistha: "O mighty-armed one, without overthinking, with a calm mind, perform those funeral rites which are to be done for this great king."
12. तदा बाढम् इति [उक्त्वा] भरतेन वसिष्ठस्य आज्ञा शिरसा [धृता]। तेन [सर्वे] ऋत्विक्-पुरोहित-आचार्याः [एतस्मै] कार्याय शीघ्रतां कर्तुम् [उक्ताः]।
**Translation:** Then, saying "Agreed," Bharata bore the command of Vashistha on his head (accepted it respectfully). By him, all the officiating priests, family priests, and preceptors were told to make haste for this task.
13. राज्ञः अग्नि-शालातः अग्नयः बहिः [आनीताः]। तेषु ऋत्विग्भिः याजकैः [विधि-पूर्वकः] होमः [कृतः]।
**Translation:** The sacred fires were brought outside from the king's fire-chamber. In them, a rule-abiding oblation was made by the officiating priests and sacrificers.
14. तत्-पश्चात् महा-राज-दशरथस्य [प्राण-हीनं] शरीरं शिबिकायाम् [उपवेश्य] परिचारकैः श्मशान-भूमिं प्रति [नीतम्]। तस्मिन् समये अश्रुभिः तेषां कण्ठः [रुद्धः]। मनसि तैः [महत्] दुःखम् [अनुभूतम्]।
**Translation:** After that, having seated the lifeless body of Great King Dasharatha in a palanquin, it was taken towards the cremation ground by the attendants. At that time, their throats were choked with tears. Great sorrow was felt by them in their minds.
15. मार्गे राज-पुरुषाः राज्ञः शवस्य पुरतः सुवर्णं रजतं [विविधानि] वस्त्राणि [विकिरन्तः] [प्रचलिताः]।
**Translation:** On the way, royal officials moved forward scattering gold, silver, and various garments in front of the king's body.

16–17. श्मशान-भूमिं [प्राप्य] चिता [सज्जी-कृता]। [कैश्चित्] जनैः चन्दन-अगरु-गुग्गुल-सरल-पद्मक-देवदारु-काष्ठानि [आनीय] चितायां [प्रक्षिप्तानि]। [अन्यैः] जनैः [विविधाः] सुगन्धि-पदार्थाः [आनीय] चितायां [समर्पिताः]। अस्य अनन्तरम् ऋत्विग्भिः राज्ञः शवः चितायाम् [स्थापितः]।
**Translation:** Having reached the cremation ground, the funeral pyre was prepared. By some people, woods of sandalwood, eaglewood, bdellium, pine, padmaka, and deodar, having been brought, were thrown onto the pyre. By other people, various fragrant substances, having been brought, were offered on the pyre. After this, the king's body was placed on the pyre by the priests.

18. तस्मिन् समये अग्नौ आहुतिं [दत्त्वा] तस्य ऋत्विग्भिः [वेद-उक्तानां] मन्त्राणां जपः [कृतः]। साम-गानं [कुर्वद्भिः] विद्वद्भिः शास्त्रीय-पद्धतिम् [अनुसृत्य] साम-श्रुतीनां गानम् [आरब्धम्]।
**Translation:** At that time, having given an oblation in the fire, the chanting of mantras mentioned in the Vedas was done by his priests. Following the scriptural method, the singing of Sama Vedic hymns was begun by the learned men singing the Sama.

19–20. तदनन्तरं चितायाम् अग्निः [प्रज्वलितः]। राज-दशरथस्य कौसल्या-प्रमुखाः राज्ञ्यः [वृद्धैः] रक्षकैः [परिवेष्टिताः] [यथा-योग्यासु] शिबिकाः रथान् [आरुह्य] नगरात् [निर्गताः]। ताभिः [शोक-सन्तप्ताभिः] श्मशान-भूमिम् [आगत्य] [अश्वमेध-यज्ञ-अनुष्ठातुः] राज-दशरथस्य शवस्य प्रदक्षिणा [कृता]। ऋत्विग्भिः अपि तस्य शवस्य प्रदक्षिणा [कृता]।
*(Corrected: शिबिकासु रथेषु → शिबिकाः रथान्)*
**Translation:** Afterwards, the fire was ignited on the pyre. The queens of King Dasharatha, headed by Kausalya, surrounded by elderly guards, having mounted appropriate palanquins and chariots, departed from the city. Having come to the cremation ground, a circumambulation of the body of King Dasharatha, the performer of the Ashvamedha sacrifice, was done by those grief-stricken ones. Circumambulation of his body was also done by the priests.

21. तस्मिन् समये तत्र [करुणं] क्रन्दनं [कुर्वतीनां] [सहस्र-शोक-आर्तानां] राज्ञीनाम् आर्त-नादः [कुररी-चीत्कार-समानः] [श्रुतः]।
**Translation:** At that time, the wailing cry of the queens crying piteously there, afflicted with a thousand sorrows, was heard like the shrieking of female ospreys.
22. दाह-कर्मणः पश्चात् [विवशाः] [रुदत्यः] ताः राज-राज्ञ्यः पौनःपुन्येन विलापं [कृत्वा] वाहनेभ्यः एव सरयू-तटं [गत्वा] [अवतीर्णाः]।
**Translation:** After the cremation rite, those helpless, weeping royal queens, having lamented repeatedly, went to the bank of the Sarayu river from their vehicles and descended.
23. भरतेन सह राज्ञीभिः मन्त्रिभिः पुरोहितैः अपि राज्ञे जल-अञ्जलिः [दत्तः]। ततः [सर्वे] नेत्राभ्याम् अश्रूणि [स्रावयन्तः] नगरम् [आगताः]। दश-दिनानि यावत् भूमौ शयनं [कुर्वद्भिः] तैः [महता] दुःखेन स्व-समयः [यापितः]।
**Translation:** An offering of water (with joined palms) was given to the king by the queens, ministers, and priests along with Bharata. Then, all of them came to the city, shedding tears from their eyes. Sleeping on the ground for ten days, their time was passed by them with great sorrow.
