**2.30.1**

* **संस्कृतम्:** रामेण [सान्त्व्यमाना] अपि जनकात्मजा मैथिली वनवासार्थम् [इदम्] भर्तारम् अवादीत्।
* **English:** Even as she was [being consoled] by Sri Rama, Janaka's daughter Maithili spoke [this] to her husband to obtain permission for forest exile.

---

**2.30.2**

* **संस्कृतम्:** [अत्यन्तं] [संविग्नया] सीतया प्रेम्णा स्वाभिमानेन च [विशालवक्षसः] राघवस्य उपरि आक्षेपः [कृतः]।
* **English:** [Deeply] [agitated], an objection was [made] by Sita against the [broad-chested] Rama out of love and self-respect.

---

**2.30.3**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **सीता -** हे राम! मम पित्रा [मिथिलाधिपेन] जनकेन जामातरं त्वां [प्राप्य] 'भवान् केवलं [पुरुषशरीरधारिणी] स्त्री अस्ति' इति किं [मतम्]।
* **English:** **Sita -** "O Rama! [Having obtained] you as a son-in-law, did my father, the [Mithila-ruling] Janaka, think: 'You are merely a [male-bodied] woman'?"

---

**2.30.4**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **सीता -** अहो! यदि अज्ञानाद् [अयम्] लोकः '[तपमाने] सूर्ये इव रामे [परं] तेजः नास्ति' इति [असत्यं] वदेत् तर्हि एतत् [महते] खेदाय स्यात्।
* **English:** **Sita -** "Alas! If out of ignorance [this] world were to speak this [falsehood]—that there is no [supreme] radiance in Rama who blazes like the [burning] sun—that would lead to [great] sorrow!"

---

**2.30.5**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **सीता -** हे नाथ! भवान् किं [विचिन्त्य] [विषण्णः] [सञ्जातः]। भवतः कस्मात् भयम् अस्ति। भवता एकमात्रं [भवदेकाश्रितायाः] मम परित्यागः [विचारितः]।
* **English:** **Sita -** "My Lord! [Thinking] what have you [become] [dejected]? From whom do you have fear? The abandonment of me, who am [solely dependent on you], has been [considered] by you."

---

**2.30.6**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **सीता -** सावित्री [द्युमत्सेनपुत्रं] [वीरं] सत्यवन्तम् [अनुगता]। भवान् अपि मां तथैव [स्ववशवर्तिनीं] जानातु।
* **English:** **Sita -** "Just as Savitri [followed] the [heroic] Satyavan, son of Dyumatsena, you too must know me to be [submissive to your will] in the same manner."

---

**2.30.7**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **सीता -** हे [अनघ] रघुनन्दन! [अन्या] [कुलकलङ्किनी] स्त्री [परपुरुषं] प्रति दृष्टिं स्थापयति। अहम् [तादृशी] न अस्मि। मया भवन्तं विना [अन्यः] पुरुषः मनसा अपि न [दृष्टः]। अतः मया भवता सह एव [गन्तव्यम्]।
* **English:** **Sita -** "O [sinless] scion of Raghu! [Another], [disgraceful] woman casts her gaze upon [another] man. I am not [such]. By me, [no other] man has been [seen] even in mind without you. Therefore, departure must be [undertaken] by me only with you."

---

**2.30.8**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **सीता -** हे राम! [कौमारवयसि] एव भवता सह मम परिणयः [कृतः]। अहम् [चिरकालं] भवता सह [उषिता] [सती] पत्नी अस्मि। [शैलूषेण] इव मां परेभ्यः दातुं भवता सङ्कल्पः [कृतः]।
* **English:** **Sita -** "O Rama! In [early youth] itself my marriage was [solemnized] with you. I am your [chaste] wife who has [dwelt] with you for a long time. Like an actor, a resolve has been [made] by you to hand me over to others."

---

**2.30.9**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **सीता -** हे [निष्पाप] रघुनन्दन! भवता भरतस्य अनुकूलतायै मह्यम् उपदेशः [दत्तः]। तदर्थम् एव भवतः राज्याभिषेकः [अवरुद्धः] [जातः]। भवान् एव तस्य भरतस्य सर्वदा [वशवर्ती] [आज्ञापालकः] च भवतु। अहम् तु कदापि तस्य [वशवर्तिनी] न भविष्यामि।
* **English:** **Sita -** "O [sinless] scion of Raghu! Advice was [given] by you to me to act favorably towards Bharata. For that alone your coronation [became] [halted]. You yourself remain forever [subordinate] and [obedient] to that Bharata. As for me, I shall never remain [subservient] to him."

---

**2.30.10**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **सीता -** भवता मां विना वनगमनं [निश्चितम्]। तत् सर्वथा [अनुचितम्] अस्ति। तपसि वने स्वर्गे च सर्वत्र मया भवता सह एव [स्थातव्यम्]।
* **English:** **Sita -** "Departing for the forest without me has been [decided] by you. That is completely [improper]. In penance, in the forest, or in heaven—everywhere I must [reside] only with you."

---

**2.30.11**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **सीता -** उद्याने सञ्चारे [सुखशयने] च क्लेशः न भवति। तथैव भवन्तम् [अनुगच्छन्त्याः] मम वनमार्गे [कश्चित्] अपि परिश्रमः न भविष्यति।
* **English:** **Sita -** "No fatigue is experienced while strolling in a garden or during [comfortable rest]. In the same way, not [any] exhaustion will be felt on the forest path by me who am [following] you."

---

**2.30.12**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **सीता -** मार्गे कुशाः काशाः शराः [कण्टकयुक्ताः] वृक्षाः च भविष्यन्ति। भवता सह [स्थितायाः] मम तेषाम् स्पर्शः [तूलवत्] [मृगचर्मवत्] च [सुखदः] भविष्यति।
* **English:** **Sita -** "On the path there will be Kusha grass, reeds, and [thorny] trees. For me [staying] with you, their touch will be [pleasant] like [cotton] and [deerskin]."

---

**2.30.13**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **सीता -** हे प्राणवल्लभ! [प्रचण्डवातोद्भूता] धूलिः मम शरीरे पतिष्यति। अहम् ताम् [उत्तमचन्दनवत्] गणयिष्यामि।
* **English:** **Sita -** "O beloved of my life! The dust [raised by fierce winds] that falls upon my body will be counted by me as [precious sandalwood]."

---

**2.30.14**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **सीता -** अहम् वनान्तरे [स्थिता] भवता सह [हरिततृणेषु] शयनं करिष्यामि। [बहुमूल्यवस्त्रास्तृतेषु] पर्यङ्केषु अपि तस्मात् [अधिकं] सुखं न भविष्यति।
* **English:** **Sita -** "[Dwelling] in the forest, I shall sleep with you on [green grasses]. Even couches [spread with precious carpets] cannot offer [greater] comfort than that."

---

**2.30.15**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **सीता -** भवान् स्वयम् [आनीय] [स्वल्पं] वा [अधिकं] वा कन्दमूलफलं मह्यं दास्यति। तत् [सर्वं] मह्यं [दिव्यामृतरससदृशं] भविष्यति।
* **English:** **Sita -** "Whatever roots and fruits—[scarce] or [plentiful]—you [having brought] yourself give to me, [all] of that will be [like divine nectar] to me."

---

**2.30.16**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **सीता -** अहम् तत्र [ऋतुसुलभानि] फलानि पुष्पाणि च [उपभुञ्जाना] निवत्स्यामि। अहम् तत्र मातरं पितरं राजभवनं च न स्मरिष्यामि।
* **English:** **Sita -** "[Partaking of] [seasonally available] fruits and flowers, I shall dwell there. I will not remember my mother, my father, or the royal palace."

---

**2.30.17**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **सीता -** तत्र भवान् मम [किञ्चित्] अपि [विप्रियम्] आचरणं न द्रक्ष्यति। मत्कारणात् भवतः मनसि [कश्चित्] अपि शोकः न भविष्यति। अहम् भवतः [भारभूता] [दुर्भरा] न भविष्यामि।
* **English:** **Sita -** "There, you will not witness even [the slightest] [displeasing] conduct of mine. On my account, there will not be [any] sorrow in your heart. I shall not become [burdensome] or [hard to support] for you."

---

**2.30.18**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **सीता -** भवता सह यत् स्थानं भवेत् तत् एव मम स्वर्गः अस्ति। भवन्तं विना यत् स्थानं भवेत् तत् [नरकसमानं] भवति। हे राम! मम [एतादृशं] [दृढं] प्रेम [ज्ञात्वा] भवान् मया सह प्रसन्नतया वनं प्रति प्रचलतु।
* **English:** **Sita -** "Whatever place is with you, that alone is heaven for me. Whatever place is without you, that becomes [equal to hell]. O Rama! [Knowing] [such] [firm] love of mine, please proceed joyfully to the forest with me."

---

**2.30.19**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **सीता -** मम मनसि वनवासस्य कष्टेभ्यः [काचित्] अपि व्यग्रता न अस्ति। यदि भवान् माम् एवं [स्थितां] वनं न नेष्यति तर्हि अहम् अद्य एव विषं पास्यामि। अहम् कदापि शत्रूणां [वशवर्तिनी] न भविष्यामि।
* **English:** **Sita -** "There is not [any] anxiety in my mind regarding the hardships of forest life. If you do not lead me, thus [situated], to the forest, then I shall drink poison today. I shall never live [subject] to enemies."

---

**2.30.20**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **सीता -** हे नाथ! भवता [परित्यक्तायाः] मम पश्चात् [महता] दुःखेन जीवनं न सम्भविष्यति। अतः भवतः गमनकाले एव मरणं मम [वरम्] अस्ति।
* **English:** **Sita -** "O Lord! For me [abandoned] by you, surviving afterwards through [immense] grief will not be possible. Therefore, embracing death at your departure is [better] for me."

---

**2.30.21**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **सीता -** अहम् भवद्वियोगस्य [इमं] शोकं [मुहूर्तमात्रम्] अपि सोढुं न शक्नोमि। अथ [दुःखितया] मया चतुर्दश वर्षाणि यावत् [अयम्] [दारुणः] शोकः कथं [सोढव्यः]।
* **English:** **Sita -** "I cannot bear [this] grief of separation from you even for a single moment. Now, how is [this] [dreadful] sorrow to be [borne] for fourteen years by me who am [grief-stricken]?"

---

**2.30.22**

* **संस्कृतम्:** एवं [शोकसन्तप्तया] सीतया [बहुकालं] यावत् [करुणः] विलापः [कृतः]। ततः [श्रान्तया] तया स्वपतिः श्रीरामः [गाढम्] [आलिङ्गितः]। तदनन्तरं तया सस्वरम् उच्चैः रोदनं [कृतम्]।
* **English:** Thus, [mournful] lamentation was [made] for a long time by Sita who was [tormented by grief]. Thereafter, [exhausted], her husband Sri Rama was [tightly] [embraced] by her, and weeping aloud was [done] by her.

* **English:** Like water [fallen] from two lotus flowers, [crystal-clear], [pure] tears [flowed forth] from both of her eyes.

**2.30.25**

* **English:** Like a lotus [pulled out] of the water, her face—[endowed with large eyes] and [radiant like the clear moon]—[became] [withered] under the anguish of tears.

**2.30.26**

* **संस्कृतम्:** दुःखेन [अचेतनाम्] इव [स्थिता] सा सीता श्रीरामेण बाहुभ्याम् [आलिङ्गिता]। तदा ताम् [आश्वासयता] श्रीरामेण [इदं] वचनम् [उक्तम्]।
* **English:** Sita, [standing] as if [senseless] with grief, was [embraced] with both arms by Sri Rama. Reassuring her, [this] word was [spoken] by Sri Rama who was [consoling] her.

**2.30.27**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **श्रीरामः -** हे देवि! तव दुःखदानेन [प्राप्तं] स्वर्गसुखम् अपि मह्यं न रोचते। [स्वयम्भुवः] ब्रह्मणः इव मम कस्मात् अपि [किञ्चित्] अपि भयं न वर्तते।
* **English:** **Sri Rama -** "O Devi! Even the bliss of heaven [obtained] by causing you sorrow is not pleasing to me. Like the [self-born] Lord Brahma, I have not even [the slightest] fear of anything."

**2.30.28**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **श्रीरामः -** हे [शुभानने]! अहम् अरण्ये तव रक्षणे सर्वथा [समर्थः] अस्मि। तथापि तव [सम्पूर्णम्] अभिप्रायम् [अज्ञात्वा] मया तव वनवासः न [सम्मतः]।
* **English:** **Sri Rama -** "O [fair-faced lady]! I am fully [capable] of protecting you in the forest. Even so, [without fully knowing] your [complete] inner wish, your forest dwelling was not [approved] by me."

---

**2.30.29**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **श्रीरामः -** हे मैथिलि! त्वं मत्सहितवनवासाय एव [सृष्टा] असि। अतः मया त्वं त्यक्तुं न [शक्या] असि। [आत्मज्ञानी] पुरुषः यथा [स्वाभाविकीं] प्रसन्नतां न त्यजति तथैव अहम् अपि त्वां न परित्यजामि।

**2.30.30**

**2.30.31**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **श्रीरामः -** हे जनकनन्दिनि! अहम् वनं न गच्छेयम् इति सर्वथा न [सम्भवति]। [सत्ययुक्तं] [पूज्यस्य] पितुः वचनम् एव मां वनं प्रति नयति।
* **English:** **Sri Rama -** "O daughter of Janaka! That I should not go to the forest is by no means [possible]. The [truth-bound] word of my [revered] father alone leads me towards the forest."

---

**2.30.32**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **श्रीरामः -** हे [सुश्रोणि]! मातापित्रोः आज्ञायाः [आज्ञाकारिता] एव पुत्रस्य धर्मः अस्ति। अहम् तयोः आज्ञायाः उल्लङ्घनं [कृत्वा] जीवितुं न शक्नोमि।
* **English:** **Sri Rama -** "O [fair-waisted one]! [Obedience] to the command of one's parents is the duty of a son. I cannot live [having transgressed] their command."

---

**2.30.33**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **श्रीरामः -** माता पिता गुरुः च [एते] [त्रयः] [स्वसेवाधीनाः] [प्रत्यक्षदेवाः] सन्ति। एतान् [अवगणय्य] [अनधीनस्य] [अप्रत्यक्षस्य] दैवस्य आराधना [विविधप्रकारैः] कथं कर्तुं [शक्यते]।
* **English:** **Sri Rama -** "Mother, father, and preceptor—[these] [three] are [living deities] [accessible through personal service]. [Disregarding] them, how is the worship of [uncontrolled], [unseen] destiny [able] to be performed in [diverse ways]?"

**2.30.34**

* **English:** **Sri Rama -** "O Sita of [auspicious eyes]! Through the service of these, dharma, artha, and kama are [attained], and worship of the three worlds becomes [accomplished]. **2.30.36**

**2.30.37**

* **English:** **Sri Rama -** "[Noble souls] [devoted to the service of parents] attain Devaloka, Gandharvaloka, Goloka, Brahmaloka, and [other] [exalted realms]."

**2.30.38**

* **English:** **Sri Rama -** "Therefore, exactly as my [revered] father, who is [established] on the [path of truth and righteousness], commands me, I desire to act. [This] indeed is [eternal] dharma."

**2.30.39**

* **English:** **Sri Rama -** "O Sita! A [firm] resolve has been [made] by you to reside in the forest with me. Therefore, regarding taking you to Dandakaranya, my [earlier thought] has now [become] [changed]."

* **English:** **Sri Rama -** "O [faultless lady] of [captivating eyes]! Permission has now been [granted] by me for your departure to the forest. O [timid one]! Follow me and, [having become] my [partner in righteousness], practice dharma."

* **English:** **Sri Rama -** "O [beloved] Sita! [This] [noble] resolve which was [made] by you to accompany me is entirely [worthy] of both my lineage and your own family."

**2.30.42**

* **संस्कृतम्:** **श्रीरामः -** हे [सुश्रोणि]! अधुना त्वं [वनवासानुकूलानां] दानादिकर्मणाम् आरम्भं कुरु। हे सीते! अस्मिन् समये त्वां विना स्वर्गसुखम् अपि मह्यं न रोचते।
* **English:** **Sri Rama -** "O [fair-waisted one]! Begin now acts of charity [suitable for forest exile]. O Sita! At this moment, even heaven's bliss without you is not pleasing to me."

**2.30.43**

* **English:** **Sri Rama -** "Distribute [precious] [excellent] items to Brahmins. Give food to mendicants [seeking a meal]. Make haste and let there not be delay."
