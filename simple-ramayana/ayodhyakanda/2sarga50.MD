### श्रीमद्वाल्मीकीयरामायणे अयोध्याकाण्डे पञ्चाशः सर्गः (सर्ग 50)

---

**2.50.1**

**Sanskrit:** [विशालस्य] [रमणीयस्य] कोसलदेशस्य सीमाम् अतीत्य [बुद्धिमता] [लक्ष्मणाग्रजेन] श्रीरामेण अयोध्यामुखीभूय [बद्धाञ्जलिना] [उक्तम्]।

**English:** Crossing the boundary of the vast and charming Kosala territory, Lakshmana’s elder brother, the wise Sri Rama, turned his face toward Ayodhya and spoke with folded hands.

---

**2.50.2**

**Sanskrit:** श्रीरामः - [ककुत्स्थकुलजैः] नृपैः [परिपालिते] [पुरीशिरोमणिभूते] अयोध्‍ये। त्वत्तः ये देवाः त्वां पालयन्ति त्वयि वसन्ति तेभ्यः अपि वनगमनाय आज्ञां प्रार्थये।

**English:** "O Ayodhya, supreme jewel among cities, protected by the kings of Kakutstha's lineage! From you and from the guardian deities who protect you and dwell within you, I seek leave to depart for the forest."

---

**2.50.3**

**Sanskrit:** श्रीरामः - वनवासकालं समाप्य महाराजस्य ऋणात् [मुक्तः] अहं पुनः आगत्य त्वां द्रक्ष्यामि। स्वपितृभ्यां सह अपि सङ्गमं करिष्यामि।

**English:** "Having completed the term of exile and being freed from the monarch's debt, I shall return to behold you again, and I shall also be reunited with my parents."

---

**2.50.4**

**Sanskrit:** ततः [सुन्दरारुणनेत्रयुक्तेन] श्रीरामेण [दक्षिणं] बाहुम् उद्यम्य नेत्राभ्याम् अश्रूणि [स्रावयता] [दुःखितेन] जानपदान् [उक्तम्]।

**English:** Then Sri Rama, possessed of lovely reddish eyes, raising his right arm, shedding tears from his eyes, and filled with distress, spoke to the people of the countryside.

---

**2.50.5**

**Sanskrit:** श्रीरामः - युष्माभिः मयि [महती] कृपा [यथोचिता] दया [कृता]। मत्कृते युष्माभिः दीर्घकालं कष्टं [सोढम्]। युष्माकं दुःखं न [समीचीनम्]। अतः इदानीं यूयं स्वानि स्वानि कार्याणि कर्तुं गच्छत।

**English:** "You have shown great benevolence and fitting compassion toward me. On my account you have endured hardships for long. Your sorrow is not proper; therefore, depart now to attend to your respective duties."

---

**2.50.6**

**Sanskrit:** एतत् श्रुत्वा तैः नरैः [महात्मानं] श्रीरामं प्रणम्य तस्य प्रदक्षिणा [कृता]। तीव्रं [विलपन्तः] ते इतस्ततः [गताः]।

**English:** Hearing this, those men bowed to the noble-souled Sri Rama, circumambulated him, and wailing loudly, dispersed in various directions.

---

**2.50.7**

**Sanskrit:** तेषां नेत्राणि श्रीरामस्य दर्शनेन न [तृप्तानि]। तैः पूर्ववत् [विलपितम्]। तावत् एव श्रीरघुनाथः तेषां दृष्टेः तथा [तिरोहितः] यथा प्रदोषकाले सूर्यः [अस्तङ्गतः]।

**English:** Their eyes remained unsated with the sight of Sri Rama, and they continued to lament as before; meanwhile, the scion of Raghu vanished from their view just as the sun sets at twilight.

---

**2.50.8–10**

**Sanskrit:** ततः [पुरुषसिंहेन] श्रीरामेण रथेन एव सः कोसलजनपदः [अतीतः]। सः [धनधान्यसम्पन्नः] [सुखदायकः]। तत्र [सर्वे] जनाः [दानशीलाः]। तस्मिन् जनपदे कुतश्चित् भयं न। तत्र भूभागाः [रमणीयाः] [चैत्यवृक्षयज्ञयूपव्याप्ताः]। [बहुभिः] उद्यानैः आम्रवनैः तस्य जनपदस्य शोभा [संवर्धिता]। तत्र [जलपूर्णैः] [बहुभिः] जलाशयैः [शोभितः]। [समग्रः] जनपदः [हृष्टपुष्टजनपूर्णः] गोसमूहैः [व्याप्तः] [सेवितः]। तत्र ग्रामाः [बहुभिः] नृपैः [रक्षिताः]। तत्र वेदमन्त्राणां ध्वनिः [अनुगुञ्जितः]।

**English:** Thereafter, Sri Rama, a lion among men, traversed in his chariot through that realm of Kosala, which was affluent with grain and wealth and pleasing. All the citizens there were munificent. In that province there was fear from no quarter. Its landscapes were delightful, filled with sacred trees and sacrificial stakes. Many orchards and mango groves heightened the region's splendor. It was adorned by numerous brimming reservoirs. The entire country was populated by contented, thriving people, and was traversed and grazed by herds of cattle. Its settlements were protected by many kings, and the resonance of Vedic chants echoed throughout.

---

**2.50.11**

**Sanskrit:** कोसलदेशात् अग्रे गत्वा [धैर्यवतां] [श्रेष्ठेन] श्रीरामचन्द्रेण मध्यमार्गेण तथाभूतं राज्यम् [अतिक्रान्तम्]। यत् [सुखसुविधायुक्तं] [धनधान्यसम्पन्नं] [रमणीयोद्यानव्याप्तं] [सामन्तनरेशोपभोग्यम्]।

**English:** Journeying onward from the Kosala country, Sri Ramachandra, foremost among the steadfast, passed along the central route through a territory endowed with conveniences, rich in provisions and wealth, covered with charming gardens, and worthy of enjoyment by tributary chiefs.

---

**2.50.12**

**Sanskrit:** तस्मिन् राज्ये श्रीरघुनाथेन [त्रिपथगामिन्याः] [दिव्यायाः] गङ्गानद्याः दर्शनं [कृतम्]। या [शीतलजलपूर्णा] [शैवलरहिता] [रमणीया] [महर्षिसेविता]।

**English:** In that realm, Sri Raghunatha gazed upon the celestial river Ganga flowing through the three realms, filled with cool water, clear of moss, lovely, and attended by venerable sages.

---

**2.50.13**

**Sanskrit:** तस्याः तटे अविदूरे [निर्मितैः] [बहुभिः] [सुन्दरैः] आश्रमैः तस्याः देवनद्याः शोभा [संवर्धिता]। काले काले अवतीर्य [हृष्टाभिः] अप्सरोभिः अपि तस्याः जलकुण्डानि [सेवितानि]। सा गङ्गा [सर्वकल्याणकारिणी]।

**English:** Near her shores, the splendor of that divine river was magnified by numerous beautiful hermitages erected at close intervals. From time to time, joyful celestial maidens descended to sport in her water basins. That Ganga bestows well-being upon all.

---

**2.50.14**

**Sanskrit:** देवैः दानवैः गन्धर्वैः किन्नरैः च तस्याः [शिवस्वरूपायाः] भागीरथ्याः शोभा [संवर्धिता]। नागानां गन्धर्वाणां च पत्नीभिः तस्याः जलं सदा [सेवितम्]।

**English:** Gods, demons, Gandharvas, and Kinnaras enhanced the grace of that auspicious Bhagirathi. The consorts of Nagas and Gandharvas regularly partook of her waters.

---

**2.50.15**

**Sanskrit:** गङ्गायाः उभयोः तटयोः देवानां [शतशः] [पर्वतीयानि] क्रीडास्थलानि [सन्ति]। तस्याः तीरे देवानां [बहूनि] उद्यानानि अपि [स्थितानि]। देवानां क्रीडार्थं सा आकाशे अपि [विद्यमाना] तत्र देवपद्मिनीति रूपेण [विख्याता]।

**English:** On both banks of the Ganga stood hundreds of mountainous sporting grounds of the gods. Along her edges were also situated many gardens of the celestials. She exists also in the firmament for the play of the immortals, renowned there as Devapadmini.

---

**2.50.16**

**Sanskrit:** प्रस्तरखण्डैः सह गङ्गाजलस्य सङ्घट्टनेन यः शब्दः जायते सः एव तस्याः [उग्रः] अट्टहासः इव। जलात् [उत्थितः] फेनः एव तस्याः [दिव्यायाः] नद्याः [निर्मलः] हासः। कुत्रचित् तस्याः जलं [वेणीरूपम्]। कुत्रचित् सा आवर्तैः [शोभिता]।

**English:** The sound produced by Ganga's water dashing against rocks was, as it were, her fierce boisterous laugh. The white foam rising from the waters was the spotless smile of that celestial river. In places her stream resembled a braided tress, and elsewhere she was adorned with whirlpools.

---

**2.50.17**

**Sanskrit:** कुत्रचित् तस्याः जलं [निश्चलं] [गम्भीरं] च। कुत्रचित् सा [महावेगेनावृता]। कुत्रचित् तस्याः जलात् [मृदङ्गादिसदृशः] [गम्भीरः] घोषः [प्रकटितः]। कुत्रचित् [वज्रपातादिसदृशः] [भयङ्करः] नादः [श्रुतः]।

**English:** In some spots her water was motionless and deep; elsewhere she rushed with tremendous momentum. Here a deep rumble akin to a mridanga drum burst forth from her waters, and there a dreadful roar like a thunderstroke was heard.

---

**2.50.18**

**Sanskrit:** तस्याः जले देवसमूहैः स्नानं [कृतम्]। कुत्रचित् तस्याः जलं [नीलोत्पलैः] कुमुदैः वा [आच्छादितम्]। कुत्रचित् [विशालं] पुलिनं [दृष्टं] कुत्रचित् तु [निर्मलः] वालुकाराशिः [दृष्टः]।

**English:** Troops of gods bathed in her waters. In places her surface was strewn with blue lotuses or water lilies. Somewhere a broad sandbank was seen, and elsewhere a stretch of pristine sand.

---

**2.50.19**

**Sanskrit:** तत्र हंसानां सारसानां च कलरवाः [गुञ्जिताः]। चक्रवाकैः तस्याः देवनद्याः शोभा [संवर्धिता]। [सदामत्तैः] विहङ्गमैः तस्याः जले [भ्रमितम्]। सा [उत्तमाङ्गरूपशोभासम्पन्ना]।

**English:** The calls of swans and cranes resounded there. Chakravaka birds enhanced the beauty of the celestial stream. Birds, perpetually joyous, hovered over her currents. She was endowed with supreme elegance of form.

---

**2.50.20**

**Sanskrit:** कुत्रचित् [तीररुहैः] वृक्षैः [मालाकारैः] [भूतैः] तस्याः शोभा [संवर्धिता]। कुत्रचित् तस्याः जलं [फुल्लोत्पलच्छन्नं] कुत्रचित् तु [पद्मवनव्याप्तम्]।

**English:** In places, trees growing along the banks formed garlanding lines to embellish her. In some stretches her waters were blanketed by blossomed blue water lilies, and in others filled with groves of lotuses.

---

**2.50.21–24**

**Sanskrit:** कुत्रचित् कुमुदसमूहैः कुत्रचित् तु कलिकाभिः सा [शोभिता]। कुत्रचित् [नानापुष्पपरागव्याप्ता] सा [मदमत्तनारीसदृशी] [लक्षिता]। तया पापराशिः [दूरीकृतः]। तस्याः जलं मणिवत् [निर्मलं] [दृष्टम्]। तस्याः तीरवर्तिवनम् [मत्तमहागजैः] [कोलाहलपूर्णं] [कृतम्]। सा फलैः पुष्पैः पल्लवैः गुल्मैः पक्षिभिः च [संवृता] [समीचीनभूषणभूषिता] युवती इव [विभाता]। तस्याः प्राकट्यं विष्णोः चरणाभ्यां [जातम्]। तस्यां पापलेशः न [दृष्टः]। सा [दिव्या] गङ्गा जीवानां [सर्वपापनाशिनी]।

**English:** Here she was adorned with water-lily clusters and there with fresh buds. In parts, coated with varied flower pollens, she resembled an intoxicated woman. She washed away the multitude of sins. Her water appeared pure and clear as crystal. The depths of the woods along her banks were made turbulent with the cries of rutting guardian tuskers, wild bull-elephants, and majestic mounts of Indra. Covered with fruits, blossoms, fresh shoots, thickets, and birds, she shone like a maiden meticulously draped in choice ornaments. Her advent occurred from the feet of Vishnu; in her not a speck of evil lingered, and that divine Ganga dispelled every sin of living souls.

---

**2.50.25–26**

**Sanskrit:** तस्याः जले शिंशुमाराः नक्राः भुजङ्गाः च निवसन्ति। [सगरकुलजेन] भगीरथेन [तपोमयेन] तेजसा शङ्करस्य जटाजूटात् तस्याः अवतरणं [जातम्]। सा समुद्रस्य महिषी। तस्याः तु समीपे सारसाः क्रौञ्चाः च कलरवं [कुर्वन्तः] [स्थिताः]। तस्याः देवनद्याः गङ्गायाः समीपं [महाबाहुः] श्रीरामः [प्राप्तः]। गङ्गायाः सा धारा शृङ्गवेरपुरे [प्रवाहिता]।

**English:** Porpoises, crocodiles, and serpents resided in her waters. By the ascetical energy of Bhagiratha of Sagara's line, her descent was effected from Shankara's matted hair. She was the consort of the ocean. In her vicinity, cranes and curlews lingered, sending forth their warblings. Near to that divine stream did the mighty-armed Sri Rama arrive. That course of the Ganga was flowing through Shringaverapura.

---

**2.50.27**

**Sanskrit:** [तरङ्गव्याप्तावर्तयुक्तां] तां गङ्गां दृष्ट्वा [महारथेन] श्रीरामेण सारथिः सुमन्त्रः [उक्तः]। श्रीरामः - सूत अद्य वयं अत्र एव वत्स्यामः।

**English:** Beholding that Ganga whose whirlpools surged with waves, Sri Rama, the great car-warrior, said to the charioteer Sumantra: "Charioteer, we shall lodge here this very day."

---

**2.50.28**

**Sanskrit:** श्रीरामः - सारथे गङ्गायाः समीपे एव [बहुपुष्पनवपल्लवशोभितः] यः अयं [महान्] इङ्गुदीवृक्षः अस्ति अस्य एव अधः अद्य रात्रौ वयं निवत्स्यामः।

**English:** "O charioteer, beneath this mighty Ingudi tree right near the Ganga, adorned with abundant flowers and young leaves, we shall dwell tonight."

---

**2.50.29**

**Sanskrit:** श्रीरामः - देवानां मनुष्याणां गन्धर्वाणां सर्पाणां पशूनां पक्षिणां च कृते यस्याः जलं [समादरणीयम्] अस्ति [कल्याणरूपायाः] नदीषु [श्रेष्ठायाः] तस्याः गङ्गायाः दर्शनं मया इतः अपि [सततं] [प्राप्तव्यम्]।

**English:** "I shall also obtain from this spot a constant view of that auspicious Ganga, the foremost of rivers, whose water is revered by gods, men, Gandharvas, serpents, beasts, and winged creatures alike."

---

**2.50.30**

**Sanskrit:** तदा लक्ष्मणेन सुमन्त्रेण च श्रीरामाय बाढम् इति उक्त्वा अश्वैः तस्य इङ्गुदीवृक्षस्य समीपं [गतम्]।

**English:** Thereupon Lakshmana and Sumantra, answering "So be it" to Sri Rama, approached that Ingudi tree with the steeds.

---

**2.50.31**

**Sanskrit:** तस्य [रमणीयस्य] वृक्षस्य समीपं गत्वा [इक्ष्वाकुनन्दनः] श्रीरामः स्वभार्यया सीतया भ्रात्रा लक्ष्मणेन च सह रथात् [अवतीर्णः]।

**English:** Approaching that lovely tree, Sri Rama, the delight of the Ikshvakus, descended from the car along with his consort Sita and brother Lakshmana.

---

**2.50.32**

**Sanskrit:** ततः सुमन्त्रेण अपि अवतीर्य [उत्तमाः] अश्वाः [मोचिताः]। वृक्षस्य मूले [उपविष्टस्य] श्रीरामचन्द्रस्य समीपं गत्वा सः [बद्धकरपुटः] [स्थितः]।

**English:** Then Sumantra also alighted and unyoked the noble horses; walking up to Sri Ramachandra who was seated at the base of the tree, he stood with folded hands.

---

**2.50.33**

**Sanskrit:** शृङ्गवेरपुरे गुहनाम्ना राज्ञा राज्यं [कृतम्]। सः श्रीरामचन्द्रस्य [प्राणसमः] [प्रियः] सखा [अभूत्]। तस्य जन्म निषादकुले [जातम्]। सः [शारीरिकसैनिकबलाभ्यां] [बलवान्] तत्रत्यानां निषादानां [सुविख्यातः] राजा च [अभूत्]।

**English:** In Shringaverapura a king named Guha ruled. He was a dear comrade of Sri Ramachandra, valued as his own breath. He was born of the Nishada race. He was powerful in physical vigor as well as martial strength, and was the celebrated chieftain of the local Nishadas.

---

**2.50.34**

**Sanskrit:** [पुरुषसिंहः] श्रीरामः मम राज्यम् [आगतः] इति यदा तेन [श्रुतम्] तदा सः [वृद्धैः] मन्त्रिभिः बान्धवैः च [संवृतः] तत्र [सम्प्राप्तः]।

**English:** When he heard that Sri Rama, a lion among men, had reached his realm, he went thither attended by elderly counselors and kinsmen.

---

**2.50.35**

**Sanskrit:** दूरात् [उपस्थितं] निषादराजं दृष्ट्वा श्रीरामेण [लक्ष्मणसहितेन] अग्रे गत्वा सः [सङ्गतः]।

**English:** Perceiving the ruler of the Nishadas approaching from afar, Sri Rama stepped ahead together with Lakshmana and received him.

---

**2.50.36**

**Sanskrit:** वल्कलादीनि [धारयन्तं] श्रीरामचन्द्रं दृष्ट्वा गुहेन [महत्] दुःखम् [अनुभूतम्]। श्रीरघुनाथं हृदयेन आलिङ्ग्य तेन [उक्तम्]। गुहः - श्रीराम यथा भवतः कृते अयोध्यायाः राज्यम् अस्ति तथैव इदं राज्यम् अपि। वदतु मया भवतः का सेवा [कार्या]। [महाबाहो] [भवत्सदृशः] [प्रियः] अतिथिः कस्य कृते [सुलभः]।

**English:** Seeing Sri Ramachandra dressed in bark garments, Guha felt acute pain. Embracing the scion of Raghu to his heart, he said: "O Rama, as the realm of Ayodhya is yours, even so is this land. Command what service I may offer you. O mighty-armed one, to whom can a guest as beloved as yourself be accessible?"

**2.50.37–39**

**Sanskrit:** ततः [नानाविधम्] [उत्तमम्] अन्नं गृहीत्वा सः सेवायाम् [उपस्थितः]। तेन शीघ्रम् अर्घ्यं [निवेदितम्] एवम् [उक्तम्] च। गुहः - [महाबाहो] भवतः स्वागतम्। ममाधिकारे [स्थिता] इयं [सकला] भूमिः भवतः एव। वयं भवतः सेवकाः भवान् अस्माकं स्वामी। अद्यप्रभृति भवता एव अस्मद्राज्यस्य [सम्यक्] शासनं [कर्तव्यम्]। इदं [भक्ष्यभोज्यपेयलेह्यम्] अन्नं भवतः सेवायाम् [उपस्थितम्]। इदं [स्वीकार्यम्]। इमाः [उत्तमोत्तमाः] शय्याः सन्ति भवतः अश्वानां भक्षणाय चणकतृणादिकम् अपि [प्रस्तुतम्]। इयं [सकला] सामग्री [स्वीकृता] भवतु।

**English:** Thereupon, bringing sundry choice victuals, he presented himself in attendance. He promptly offered the arghya reception and spoke: "O mighty-armed one, welcome to you. All this land under my sway is truly yours. We are your attendants, and you are our sovereign. From this day forward, govern this kingdom of ours justly. This fourfold food—masticable, edible, potable, and lickable—is brought for your enjoyment; please accept it. Here are comfortable couches, and gram, fodder, and forage have been readied for the nourishment of your horses; pray accept all this."

**2.50.40**

**Sanskrit:** गुहेन एवम् [उक्ते] श्रीरामचन्द्रेण तस्मै एवम् उत्तरं [दत्तम्]। श्रीरामः - सखे त्वया पादाभ्याम् अत्र [आगतेन] स्नेहं [प्रदर्शयता] सदा अस्माकं कृते [सम्यक्] पूजनस्वागतसत्कारः [कृतः]। त्वां [सङ्गतः] अहं [भृशं] [प्रसन्नः]।

**English:** When Guha spoke thus, Sri Ramachandra made this answer: "O friend, by your coming here on foot and exhibiting such warmth, our veneration, welcome, and entertainment have been amply fulfilled for all time. Meeting you has brought me intense joy."

**2.50.41–42**

**Sanskrit:** ततः श्रीरामेण [स्वकीयाभ्यां] [वर्तुलाभ्यां] भुजाभ्यां गुहं [दृढम्] आलिङ्ग्य [उक्तम्]। श्रीरामः - गुह इदं सौभाग्याय यत् अद्य त्वां बन्धुबान्धवैः सह स्वस्थम् आनन्दसहितम् पश्यामि। तव राज्ये मित्रेषु वनेषु च सर्वत्र कुशलम् अस्ति किम् इति उच्यताम्।

**English:** Then Sri Rama, clasping Guha warmly within his rounded arms, spoke: "Guha, it is my good fortune that I behold you hale and joyful amidst your kinsfolk today. Relate whether all is well throughout your kingdom, among your allies, and in the forest tracts."

---

**2.50.43**

**Sanskrit:** श्रीरामः - त्वया प्रेम्णा या काचित् इयं सामग्री [प्रस्तुता] ताम् अङ्गीकृत्य अहं त्वां तां प्रतिनेतुम् आज्ञापयामि। यतः अस्मिन् समये अन्यैः [दत्तं] किमपि वस्तु अहं न गृह्णामि न च उपयुञ्जे।

**English:** "Whatever provisions you brought out of affection, having acknowledged them, I instruct you to take them back; for at this juncture I neither receive nor make use of any offering made by others."

---

**2.50.44**

**Sanskrit:** श्रीरामः - वल्कलं मृगचर्म च [धारयन्] अहं फलमूलाहारं करोमि धर्मे [स्थितः] च सन् [तापसवेषेण] वनस्य अन्तः एव विचरामि। अस्मिन् काले मां त्वम् अस्मिन् एव नियमे [स्थितं] जानीहि।

**English:** "Donning tree bark and deer pelt, I subsist on fruits and roots; anchored in righteousness, I wander purely within the forest in the garb of an anchorite. In this period, consider me dedicated to this austere vow."

---

**2.50.45**

**Sanskrit:** श्रीरामः - एतासु सामग्रीषु यत् अश्वानां [खाद्यपेयवस्तु] अस्ति तस्य एव अस्मिन् काले मम आवश्यकता अस्ति न तु [अन्योत्तमवस्तुनः]। अश्वेभ्यः खाद्यपेयदानमात्रेण एव त्वया मम [पूर्णः] सत्कारः करिष्यते।

**English:** "Among these items, whatever serves for the sustenance and drink of the horses, of that alone have I any need at this hour, and of nothing else. Solely through the provision of fodder and water for the horses shall my complete hospitality be discharged by you."

---

**2.50.46**

**Sanskrit:** श्रीरामः - एते अश्वाः मम पितुः महाराजदशरथस्य [अतिप्रियाः]। एतेषां खाद्यपेययोः [सुन्दरे] प्रबन्धे कृते मम [सम्यक्] पूजनं भविष्यति।

**English:** "These chargers are cherished by my sire, Emperor Dasharatha. By arranging properly for their provender and drink, my own honoring will be fully achieved."

---

**2.50.47**

**Sanskrit:** तदा गुहेन [स्वकीयाः] सेवकाः तस्मिन् एव समये [आज्ञप्ताः]। यूयम् अश्वानां खाद्यपेयार्थम् [आवश्यकानि] वस्तूनि शीघ्रम् आनीय दत्त।

**English:** Then Guha forthwith commanded his retainers: "Fetch and furnish without delay the provisions required for the nourishment and watering of the horses."

---

**2.50.48**

**Sanskrit:** तत्पश्चात् [वल्कलचीरोत्तरीयधारिणा] श्रीरामेण सायं सन्ध्योपासनां कृत्वा भोजनरूपेण [लक्ष्मणानीतं] [केवलं] जलमात्रं [पीतम्]।

**English:** Afterwards, Sri Rama, wearing an upper garment made of bark, concluded his evening twilight rites and, by way of food, partook solely of the water fetched by Lakshmana himself.

---

**2.50.49**

**Sanskrit:** ततः [भार्यासहितेन] श्रीरामेण भूमौ एव [तृणशय्याम्] आस्तीर्य [सुप्तम्]। तस्मिन् काले लक्ष्मणेन तस्य पादौ प्रक्षाल्य प्रमृज्य च ततः किञ्चिद्दूरम् अपसृत्य [वृक्षाश्रितेन] [उपविष्टम्]।

**English:** Then Sri Rama, alongside his consort, slept upon the bare earth after spreading a pallet of grass. At that time Lakshmana, having washed and dried his brother's feet, withdrew to a short distance and sat leaning against a tree.

---

**2.50.50**

**Sanskrit:** गुहेन अपि [सावधानेन] धनुः धृत्वा सुमन्त्रेण सह उपविश्य [सौमित्रिणा] लक्ष्मणेन सह [सम्भाषमाणेन] श्रीरामस्य रक्षणार्थं [सकलां] रात्रिं [जागरितम्]।

**English:** Guha too, vigilantly grasping his bow, sat beside Sumantra and, conversing with Sumitra's son Lakshmana, kept awake throughout the night for the protection of Sri Rama.

**2.50.51**

**Sanskrit:** एवं [सुप्तस्य] [यशस्विनः] [मनस्विनः] [दशरथनन्दनस्य] [महात्मनः] श्रीरामस्य सा रात्रिः बहुकालानन्तरं [व्यतीता]। येन कदापि दुःखं न [दृष्टम्]। यः सुखभोगयोग्यः [अभूत्]।

**English:** Thus, for the renowned, high-minded descendant of Dasharatha, the illustrious Rama, who had never known misery and was worthy solely of comfort, the night slowly reached its close as he lay at rest.
