### Verses 1–2

**Sanskrit:** [पराक्रमी] भरतः राज-गृहात् निर्गत्य [पूर्वस्याम्] दिशि [गतः] । सः [तेजस्वी] राज-कुमारः मार्गे सुदामा-नदीम् दृष्ट्वा ताम् [तीर्णः] । ततः [श्रीमान्] इक्ष्वाकु-नन्दनः [विस्तृत-तटाम्] ह्रादिनी-नदीम् उल्लङ्घ्य [पश्चिम-वाहिनीम्] शतद्रु-नदीम् [तीर्णः] ।

**English:** Departing from Rajagriha, the valiant Bharata headed in the eastern direction. The radiant prince saw and crossed the Sudama river on the way. Thereafter, the glorious descendant of Ikshvaku, Bharata, crossed the wide-banked Hradini river and traversed the west-flowing Shatadru river.

### Verse 3

**Sanskrit:** ततः [ऐलधान-नामकम्] ग्रामम् गत्वा तत्र [वहन्तीम्] नदीम् सः [तीर्णः] । तत्-पश्चात् सः [अपरपर्वत-नामकम्] जनपदम् [गतः] । तत्र [शिला-नाम्नी] नदी अभूत् । सा [स्व-जल-मध्य-पतितानि] वस्तूनि [शिला-स्वरूपाणि] करोति । ताम् नदीम् तीर्त्वा भरतः आग्नेय-कोणे [स्थितम्] [शल्यकर्षण-नामकम्] देशम् [गतः] । तत्र [शरीर-कण्टक-निष्कासन-साहाय्य-कारिण्यः] ओषध्यः [उपलब्धाः] अभूवन् ।

**English:** Going from there to the village named Ailadhana, he crossed the river flowing there. Afterwards, he went to the district named Aparaparvata. There was a river named Shila which turned objects fallen into its waters into stone. Crossing that river, Bharata went to the country named Shalyakarshana situated in the southeast, where herbs helping to extract thorns from the body were found.

**English:** Thereafter, the river named Shilavaha was seen by the purified, truth-pledged Bharata. That river with its fierce current carries even huge rock fragments. Seeing that river, he moved forward. Crossing huge mountains, he reached the forest named Chaitraratha.

**English:** Thereafter, from the confluence of the west-flowing Saraswati and the stream of the Ganga, he entered the northern regions of the Viramatsya country. Going forward from there, he entered the Bharunda forest.

### Verses 7–8

**Sanskrit:** [श्रान्तान्] अश्वान् स्नापयित्वा तेषाम् अङ्गेभ्यः शीतलताम् प्रदाय छायायाम् घासादिकम् दत्त्वा भरतः स्वयम् अपि स्नानम् जल-पानम् च कृत्वा मार्गाय जलम् नीत्वा अग्रे [प्रस्थितः] । तस्मिन् [विशाले] वने मनुष्याणाम् निवासः आगमनम् वा प्रायः न अभूत् । [मङ्गलाचार-युक्तः] सः मङ्गल-रथेन आकाशम् वायुः इव तत् [विशालम्] वनम् वेग-पूर्वकम् [तीर्णः] ।

**English:** Bathing the tired horses, cooling their limbs, and giving them grass in the shade, Bharata himself also bathed, drank water, took water for the journey, and set forth. In that vast forest, the habitation or coming of humans hardly existed. Endowed with auspicious conduct, he swiftly crossed that vast forest in his auspicious chariot, just as the wind traverses the sky.

**English:** Bharata - After seeing the city of Ayodhya in front, his charioteer was addressed by Bharata thus: "O charioteer, adorned with sacred gardens, this glorious city does not appear very joyful to me today. That very Ayodhya currently looks from afar like a mound of white clay."

### Verse 34

**Sanskrit:** भरतस्य हृदयम् [एकाग्रम्] न अभूत् । सः [उद्विग्नः] अभूत् । अतः आत्मना सह [आगताः] द्वार-पालाः तेन [रघुकुल-नन्दनेन] भरतेन सत्कार-पूर्वकम् [प्रत्यावर्तिताः] । ततः तेन तत्र केकय-राजस्य अश्वपतेः [श्रान्तः] सारथिः एवम् [उक्तः] ।

**English:** Bharata's heart was not focused. He was anxious. Therefore, the gatekeepers who had come along with him were respectfully sent back by that Bharata, the delight of the Raghu clan. Then, the tired charioteer of Ashvapati, the king of Kekaya, was addressed thus by him there.

### Verse 35

**Sanskrit:** भरतः - हे [निष्पाप] सूत अहम् कारणम् विना एव एतादृश्या शीघ्रतया किमर्थम् [आहूतः] [अस्मि] । अस्याः वार्तायाः विचारस्य अनन्तरम् मम हृदये अशुभस्य आशङ्का उद्भवति । मम [दीनता-रहितः] स्वभावः अपि स्व-स्थित्याः [भ्रष्टः] इव भवति ।

**English:** Bharata - "O sinless charioteer, why have I been summoned with such haste even without a cause? After reflection upon this matter, fear of misfortune arises in my heart. Even my noble nature, devoid of depression, becomes as if fallen from its steadfast state."

### Verse 36

**Sanskrit:** भरतः - हे सारथे इतः पूर्वम् मया राज-विनाशस्य [विविधानि] लक्षणानि [श्रुतानि] । अद्य अहम् अत्र तानि [सर्वाणि] लक्षणानि पश्यामि ।

**English:** Bharata - "O charioteer, before this, various signs of the downfall of kings were heard by me. Today I see all those signs here."

### Verses 37–38 ½

**Sanskrit:** भरतः - अहम् पश्यामि गृहस्थानाम् गृहेषु सम्मार्जनम् न [कृतम्] । तानि [रूक्षाणि] [श्री-हीनानि] दृश्यन्ते तेषाम् कपाटानि च [उद्घाटितानि] सन्ति । एतेषु गृहेषु बलि-वैश्वदेव-कर्माणि न क्रियन्ते । एतानि धूपस्य सुगन्धेन [वञ्चितानि] सन्ति । एतेषु [निवसद्भिः] कुटुम्बि-जनैः भोजनम् न [प्राप्तम्] अपि च एतानि [सर्वाणि] गृहाणि [प्रभा-हीनानि] दृश्यन्ते । एतेषु लक्ष्म्याः निवासः न अस्ति इति प्रतीयते ।

**English:** Bharata - "I see that in the houses of householders sweeping has not been done. They look desolate and lusterless, and their door-panels are left open. In these houses, offerings and Vaishvadeva rituals are not performed. These are deprived of the fragrance of incense. Food has not been obtained by the family members living in them, and all these houses look gloomy. It appears that the residence of Lakshmi is not in them."

### Verse 39 ½

**Sanskrit:** भरतः - देव-मन्दिराणि पुष्पैः [सज्जितानि] न दृश्यन्ते । तेषाम् अङ्गणानि [सम्मार्जितानि] न सन्ति । एतानि मनुष्यैः [शून्यानि] भवन्ति अतः एतेषाम् पूर्वम् इव शोभा न वर्तते ।

**English:** Bharata - "The temples do not appear decorated with flowers. Their courtyards are not swept. They are empty of people, therefore their beauty as before does not exist."

### Verses 40–41 ½

**Sanskrit:** भरतः - देव-प्रतिमानाम् पूजा [निरुद्धा] [जाता] । यज्ञ-शालासु यज्ञाः न क्रियन्ते । पुष्पाणाम् मालानाम् च विपणिषु अद्य [विक्रयण-योग्यानि] वस्तूनि न शोभन्ते । अत्र पूर्वम् इव वणिजः अपि अद्य न दृश्यन्ते । चिन्तया तेषाम् हृदयम् [उद्विग्नम्] दृश्यते अपि च स्व-व्यापारे [नष्टे] [जाते] ते [सङ्कुचिताः] भवन्ति ।

**English:** Bharata - "The worship of deity idols has ceased. Sacrifices are not performed in sacrificial halls. In the markets of flowers and garlands, goods fit for sale do not shine today. Merchants too are not seen here today as before. With anxiety their hearts appear distressed, and their trade having been ruined, they have become withdrawn."

### Verses 42–43

**Sanskrit:** भरतः - देवालयेषु चैत्य-वृक्षेषु च येषाम् निवासः अस्ति ते पशु-पक्षिणः [दीनाः] दृश्यन्ते । नगरस्य [सर्वेषाम्] स्त्री-पुरुषाणाम् मुखानि [मलिनानि] सन्ति इति अहम् पश्यामि । तेषाम् नेत्राणि [अश्रु-पूर्णानि] सन्ति । अपि च ते [सर्वे] [दीनाः] [चिन्तिताः] [दुर्बलाः] [उत्कण्ठिताः] च सन्ति ।

**English:** Bharata - "The beasts and birds whose dwelling is in temples and sacred trees appear miserable. I see that the faces of all women and men of the city are gloomy. Their eyes are filled with tears. And they all are miserable, anxious, frail, and deeply distressed."

### Verse 44

**Sanskrit:** सारथिम् एवम् उक्त्वा भरतः अयोध्यायाम् [विद्यमानानि] तानि [अनिष्ट-सूचकानि] चिह्नानि [पश्यन्] मनसि [दुःखितः] अभूत् । ततः [दुःख-पूर्णेन] मनसा सः राज-भवनम् [गतः] ।

**English:** Having spoken thus to the charioteer, Bharata, beholding those ill-boding signs present in Ayodhya, became grieved in mind. Then, with a sorrow-filled mind, he went to the royal palace.

### Verse 45

**Sanskrit:** या अयोध्या-पुरी पूर्वम् देव-राजस्य इन्द्रस्य नगरी इव [शोभिता] अभूत् तस्याः चत्वराणि गृहाणि मार्गाः च अद्य [शून्यानि] अभूवन् । द्वाराणाम् कपाटानि [धूलि-धूसराणि] [जातानि] । तस्याः [एतादृशीम्] दुर्दशाम् दृष्ट्वा भरतः पूर्णतया दुःखे [निमग्नः] [जातः] ।

**English:** That city of Ayodhya which formerly was resplendent like the city of Indra, the king of the gods—its squares, houses, and streets had today become desolate. The door leaves of the gates had become grey with dust. Seeing such a wretched state of hers, Bharata became completely immersed in grief.

### Verse 46

**Sanskrit:** तस्मिन् नगरे याः पूर्वम् कदापि न [जाताः] अभूवन् [तादृशीषु] [अप्रियासु] वार्तासु [दृष्टासु] [महात्मना] भरतेन स्व-मस्तकम् [अवनतम्] [कृतम्] । तस्य हर्षः [छिन्नः] [जातः] । अपि च सः [दीन-हृदयः] पितुः भवनम् [प्रविष्टः] ।

**English:** In that city, after seeing such unpleasant occurrences that had never happened before, his head was bowed down by the noble Bharata. His joy was stripped away. And with a sorrowful heart, he entered his father's palace.
