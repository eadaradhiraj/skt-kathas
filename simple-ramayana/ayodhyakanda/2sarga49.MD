**2.49.1**
**Sanskrit:** [पुरुषसिंहः] श्रीरामः पितृशासनं पौनःपुन्येन [स्मरन्] [तस्याम्] [अवशिष्टायाम्] रात्रौ सुदूरं [गतः] ।
**English:** Rama, a lion among men, constantly remembering his father's command, went very far during the remaining part of that night itself.

**2.49.2**
**Sanskrit:** तथा [गच्छतः] तस्य [सा] [कल्याणी] रजनी [व्यतीता] । प्रभाते [मङ्गलकरं] सन्ध्योपासनं कृत्वा सः [विभिन्नान्] जनपदान् [अतिक्रामन्] [प्रस्थितः] ।
**English:** Walking in that manner, that auspicious night of his passed. When morning came, after performing the auspicious sandhya worship, he departed, crossing various territories.

**2.49.3**
**Sanskrit:** [कृष्टसीमान्तान्] ग्रामान् [पुष्पितानि] वनानि [पश्यन्] सः [उत्तमैः] अश्वैः शीघ्रं [गतः] । तथापि [रम्यदृश्यदर्शनासक्तेन] तेन रथवेगः [मन्दः] इव [ज्ञातः] ।
**English:** Seeing the villages whose border lands were ploughed and the forests adorned with flowers, he was advancing rapidly by means of those excellent horses; nevertheless, by him who was engrossed in seeing the beautiful sights, the speed of that chariot seemed slow.

**2.49.4**
**Sanskrit:** मार्गे लघुबृहद्ग्रामेषु [निवसतां] जनानाम् [इमाः] वाचः तेन [श्रुताः] ।
ग्रामजनाः - अहो [कामवश-गतं] दशरथं धिक् ।
**English:** The following words of the people living in the large and small villages on the way were heard by him: 'Alas! Woe to King Dasharatha, who has fallen under the sway of lust!

**2.49.5**
**Sanskrit:** ग्रामजनाः - हा हा [पापशीला] [पापासक्ता] [क्रूरा] [त्यक्तधर्ममर्यादा] कैकेयी दयया अपि न [स्पृष्टा] । [सा] [क्रूरा] इदानीं [निष्ठुरकर्मनिरता] [जाता] ।
**English:** 'Alas! Alas! Kaikeyi, who is of sinful nature, attached to sin, cruel, and who abandons the bounds of dharma, has not even been touched by mercy. That cruel woman is now engaged only in ruthless deeds.

**2.49.6**
**Sanskrit:** ग्रामजनाः - तया राज्ञः [धर्मात्मा] [महाज्ञानी] [दयालुः] [जितेन्द्रियः] पुत्रः वनवासाय गृहात् [निष्कासितः] ।
**English:** 'By her, the king's son, who is so righteous, highly wise, compassionate, and has conquered his senses, has been expelled from the house for dwelling in the forest.

**2.49.7**
**Sanskrit:** ग्रामजनाः - [सदासुखरता] [महाभागा] जनकनन्दिनी सीता अधुना वनवासदुःखानि कथं सहिष्यते ।
**English:** 'The illustrious daughter of Janaka, Sita, who was always accustomed to comforts, how will she now endure the miseries of forest life?

**2.49.8**
**Sanskrit:** ग्रामजनाः - अहो राजा दशरथः पुत्रं प्रति [स्नेहहीनः] [जातः] । येन प्रजानाम् [अपराधहीनः] श्रीरामचन्द्रः अत्र [त्यक्तुम्] [इष्टः] ।
**English:** 'Alas! King Dasharatha has become so devoid of affection for his son, that Sri Ramachandra, who has committed no offense against the subjects, is wished to be abandoned here.'

**2.49.9**
**Sanskrit:** [लघुबृहद्ग्रामवासिनां] जनानाम् [एताः] वाचः [शृण्वन्] [वीरः] [कोसलपतिः] श्रीरामः कोसलजनपदसीमाम् [अतिक्रम्य] अग्रे [गतः] ।
**English:** Hearing these words of the people living in small and large villages, the heroic lord of Kosala, Sri Rama, having crossed the boundary of the Kosala territory, moved forward.

**2.49.10**
**Sanskrit:** तदनन्तरं [शीतलसुखजलवाहिनीं] वेदश्रुतिनदीं तीर्त्वा श्रीरामचन्द्रः [अगस्त्य-न्युषितां] दक्षिणदिशं प्रति [प्रस्थितः] ।
**English:** Thereafter, having crossed the river named Vedashruti, which flowed with cool and pleasant water, Sri Ramachandra proceeded towards the southern direction inhabited by Agastya.

**2.49.11**
**Sanskrit:** दीर्घकालं चलित्वा तेन [शीतलजलस्रोतोवाहिनी] [समुद्रगामिनी] गोमतीनदी [तीर्णा] । तस्याः तीरे [बह्व्यः] गावः [विचरिताः] ।
**English:** After walking for a long time, he crossed the sea-bound Gomati river, which flowed with a stream of cool water. Many cows used to roam on its banks.

**2.49.12**
**Sanskrit:** श्रीरघुनाथेन [शीघ्रगामिभिः] अश्वैः गोमतीनदीं लङ्घयित्वा [मयूरहंसकलरवव्याप्ता] स्यन्दिकानदी अपि [तीर्णा] ।
**English:** By Sri Raghunath, using swift horses, having crossed the Gomati river, the river named Syandika, filled with the sounds of peacocks and swans, was also crossed.

**2.49.13**
**Sanskrit:** तत्र गत्वा श्रीरामेण सीतयै [धनधान्यसम्पन्ना] [अनेकावान्तरजनपदवेष्टिता] भूमिः [दर्शिता] । या पूर्वकाले राज्ञा मनुना इक्ष्वाकवे [दत्ता] ।
**English:** Going there, the land rich in wealth and grains and surrounded by many inner territories, which was given by King Manu to Ikshvaku in ancient times, was shown to Sita by Sri Rama.

**2.49.14**
**Sanskrit:** ततः [श्रीमता] [पुरुषोत्तमेन] श्रीरामेण हे सूत इति उक्त्वा सारथिः पौनःपुन्येन [सम्बोधितः] । तेन [मदमत्तहंसतुल्येन] [मधुरेण] स्वरेण [एवम्] [उक्तम्] ।
**English:** Then, by the glorious Purushottama Sri Rama, having said "O Suta!", the charioteer was repeatedly addressed, and he spoke in this manner in a sweet voice like an intoxicated swan—

**2.49.15**
**Sanskrit:** श्रीरामः - हे सूत कदा अहं प्रत्यागत्य मातापितरौ द्रक्ष्यामि । कदा सरयूसमीपस्थे [पुष्पिते] वने मृगयार्थं भ्रमिष्यामि ।
**English:** 'O Suta! When will I return and meet my parents, and when in the blossoming forest adjacent to Sarayu will I roam for hunting?

**2.49.16**
**Sanskrit:** श्रीरामः - सरयूवने मृगयार्थं मम [महान्] अभिलाषः न वर्तते । लोके [एषा] [अनुपमा] क्रीडा [राजर्षिसमुदायाभिमता] वर्तते ।
**English:** 'I do not have a very strong desire to hunt in the forest of Sarayu. This is a kind of matchless sport in the world, which is approved by the community of royal sages.

**2.49.17**
**Sanskrit:** श्रीरामः - अस्मिन् लोके वनं गत्वा मृगया राजर्षीणां क्रीडायै [प्रचलिता] अभूत् । अतः तदा मनुपुत्रैः [कृता] [इयं] क्रीडा [अन्यधनुर्धराणाम्] अपि [अभीष्टा] अभूत् ।
**English:** 'In this world, having gone to the forest, hunting became prevalent for the sport of royal sages. Therefore, this sport played by the sons of Manu at that time became desired by other archers too.'

**2.49.18**
**Sanskrit:** [इक्ष्वाकुनन्दनः] श्रीरामचन्द्रः [विभिन्नान्] विषयान् अधिकृत्य [मधुरया] वाण्या सूताय [उचितानि] वचनानि [कथयन्] तस्मिन् मार्गे अग्रे [गतः] ।
**English:** The joy of the Ikshvakus, Sri Ramachandra, regarding various subjects, saying appropriate things to the charioteer in a sweet voice, kept advancing on that path.
