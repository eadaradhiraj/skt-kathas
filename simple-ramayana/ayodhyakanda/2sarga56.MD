**2.56.35**

**English:** Chitrakoot mountain was exceedingly delightful. There flowed the river Malyavati (Mandakini), adorned with excellent holy bathing places and frequented by many beasts and birds. Having obtained the proximity of that mountain and river, Sri Ramachandra experienced immense joy and delight, and he forgot the distress arising from coming to the forest far away from the city.

**Sanskrit:** चित्रकूट-पर्वतः [अतीव-रमणीयः] अभूत्। तत्र [उत्तम-तीर्थ-सुशोभितया] [बहु-पशु-पक्षि-सेवितया] माल्यवत्या नद्या [प्रवाहितम्] अभूत्। तयोः पर्वत-नद्योः सान्निध्यं प्राप्य श्री-रामचन्द्रः [अतीव-हर्षितः] [आनन्दितः] अभूत्। नगरात् दूरं वनम् आगमनेन [सञ्जातं] कष्टं तेन [विस्मृतम्]।
